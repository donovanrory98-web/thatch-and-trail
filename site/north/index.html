Yorkshire vineyards you can visit

Yorkshire vineyards, from the Pennines to the Vale of York. 5 estates so far, and every one has confirmed its details with us by email: opening days, tours, food, dogs and how to get there. Hours change with the seasons, so check with the estate before you travel.
