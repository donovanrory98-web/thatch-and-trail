Christmas at the vineyard: markets, tastings and gifts

Christmas markets, festive tastings, late cellar-door opening and experiences to give for next year, at vineyards across England and Wales. Everything here was confirmed to us by the estate itself, unless it’s marked as coming from their website. Dates that have passed drop off the list.

Christmas at the vineyard: common questions

Which vineyards have Christmas markets in 2026?

Dunesforde near York holds Christmas Fairs on 28–29 November; Davenport’s HoVEC Christmas Market at Hazel Street Farm is on Saturday 5 December; Kinsbrook’s Christmas Market is on 5–6 December; and Chet Valley and Sedlescombe Organic also hold Christmas markets, so check with them for dates.

Which vineyards are open between Christmas and New Year?

Polgoon’s shop opens 10am to 3pm from 24 December, apart from Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day; Valley Farm’s Cabin-in-the-Vines is open over Christmas and New Year; and Denbies’ Vineyard Hotel is open all year, including Christmas Day. Many cellar doors close for part of the holidays, so check before you travel.

Can you buy a vineyard experience as a Christmas present?

Yes. Limeburn Hill sells its 2027 tours as gift vouchers, Saddle Goose sells its six-wine tasting as a voucher, and Walsingham’s 2027 Crypt Tastings and Pattingham’s 2027 tours are already on sale.
