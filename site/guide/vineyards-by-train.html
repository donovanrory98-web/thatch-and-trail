Vineyards you can reach by train in Sussex and Kent

Nobody wants to nominate a driver. Every vineyard in Sussex, plus the Kent estates further down, grouped by the station you'd actually get off at — with the drive from the platform. Pulborough serves seven on its own, which makes it the most useful station in English wine.
