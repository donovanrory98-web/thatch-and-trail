Vineyards open in winter in Sussex & Kent

Most vineyard days get planned for summer, but plenty of estates keep going through the colder months, and a few save their best for them. Everything in the first two sections was confirmed to us by the estate itself.

Open through the winter

Sunday roasts and long lunches

  • Tinwood Estate

    Roast dinners on Sundays throughout the winter, which the estate describes as its most loved event.

  • Kinsbrook Vineyard

    The restaurant stays open Thursday to Sunday, with Sunday roasts taking centre stage.

  • Artelium

    The Kitchen serves lunch and dinner on Fridays and Saturdays and lunch on Sundays, with a monthly tasting-menu takeover.

Tours that keep going

  • Gusbourne Estate, Kent

    All tours run year-round, except on Tuesdays. Book ahead.

  • Ridgeview Wine Estate

    Guided tours run all year (18+), including Cellar Tours. Best booked ahead.

  • Ambriel

    Tastings and tours almost every day of the year; free times show on the booking page.

  • Oastbrook Estate

    From November through to spring, winery tours run on Saturdays.

  • Artelium

    Public tours at 11am in winter, Friday to Sunday.

  • Court Garden Vineyard

    The Autumn & Winter Winery Experience, with a vermouth spritz, runs through to the end of March.

Just drop in

  • Gusbourne Estate, Kent

    Walk-ins are welcome daily at The Nest, 10am to 5pm, and the self-guided vineyard walk needs no booking.

  • Domaine Evremond, Kent

    Walk-ins are always welcome at the cellar door, from 11am, Wednesday to Sunday.

  • Sedlescombe Organic

    Open through the winter, Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, with vineyard and woodland walks.

  • Oxney Organic Estate

    Wine flights run Tuesday to Saturday from November to April.

  • Bolney Wine Estate

    Welcomes visitors year-round.

  • Beacon Down Vineyard

    The café and shop open Tuesday to Sunday, though they may close a little earlier in winter.

Cosy evenings

  • Mousehall

    Raclette evenings in autumn and winter.

  • Artelium

    Open until 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays in winter.

Winter visits: common questions

Which Sussex vineyards are open in winter?

Estates that have confirmed winter visiting with us include Tinwood (Sunday roasts), Kinsbrook (restaurant Thursday to Sunday), Artelium (Friday to Sunday), Ridgeview and Ambriel (tours and tastings through the year), Oastbrook (Saturday winery tours), Court Garden (a winter winery experience to the end of March), Sedlescombe Organic, Oxney Organic, Bolney and the Beacon Down café.

Are there Christmas markets at Sussex vineyards?

Yes. Kinsbrook’s annual Christmas Market is on 5–6 December, and Sedlescombe Organic holds a Christmas Craft Market; ask the estate for its date.

Can you visit Kent vineyards in winter?

Yes. Gusbourne takes walk-ins every day at The Nest and runs tours all year except Tuesdays, and Domaine Evremond welcomes walk-ins from 11am, Wednesday to Sunday.
