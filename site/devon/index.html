Devon vineyards to visit

Bantham
Verified

Estate-verified: A South Devon vineyard by the beach at Bantham, with a vine-to-wine tour and tasting, a horse box bar on the beach in the holidays and summer weekends, and Saturday evening Afterglow sessions.

Estate-verified: A vineyard farm near Honiton in East Devon with Saturday morning tours from April to October, late opening on Fridays and Saturdays, and wood-fired dinners, sauna sessions and Forest School planned for next summer.

Common questions

How many vineyards can you visit in Devon?

7 vineyards in Devon welcome visitors, and 7 of them have confirmed their details with Thatch & Trail directly.

Which Devon vineyards serve food?

4 have food on site, including Alder Vineyard, Heron Farm, Kenton Park Estate, Sandridge Barton Wines.

Where are the vineyards in Devon?

River valleys, the South Hams and the edges of Dartmoor and the Exe estuary. Towns with a vineyard nearby include Bantham, Clayhanger, near Tiverton, Kenton, near Exeter, Lewdown, near Okehampton, Weston, near Honiton, near Axminster, near Totnes.
