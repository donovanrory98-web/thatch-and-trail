Hampshire

Candover Brook

Candover Valley, near Basingstoke · Hampshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A small Hampshire vineyard in the Candover Valley where every tour is private, arranged just for your group, plus occasional open days.

The visit

Candover Brook is a private-tour estate: there are no public tour slots, so email to arrange a visit for your own group.

Confirmed by the estate

Private tours only

All tours are private, arranged just for you and your group. Email vineyard@candoverbrook.co.uk to book.

Confirmed by the estate

Open days

There are no open-day dates for next year yet.

What’s on

Where to find their wines

Candover Brook hopes to pour at Hampshire favourites next year, such as the Watercress Festival, glasshouse events and Trout ’n About, though places aren’t confirmed yet.

Getting there

Basingstoke station is about 15 minutes by taxi; Winchester about 25 minutes.
