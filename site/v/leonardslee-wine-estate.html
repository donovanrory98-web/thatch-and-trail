Horsham & the Arun Valley

Leonardslee Wine Estate

Lower Beeding / Horsham · West Sussex

Estate-verified · August 2026

Two estates in one: historic lakes and gardens open seven days a week, and a wine estate where the vines sit surrounded by forest — and you drive your own electric golf buggy out to them.

The visit

Leonardslee is really two properties either side of the road — Leonardslee Lakes & Gardens, and Leonardslee Wine Estate — each with its own signature wine experience, its own hotel, and a shared reputation as one of the more spectacular days out in West Sussex. Both properties carry a tourism offering from 1 October 2026.

The estate’s own tip

Brunch, Tour & Tasting — and the golf buggy

The estate’s pick for a first visit is the Brunch, Tour & Tasting at Leonardslee Wine Estate (£49.50pp). Vineyards surrounded by forest are rare enough on their own — but you also drive your own electric golf buggy out to the vines, which is genuinely unlike anywhere else in Sussex.

Over at the Gardens

The Gardens to Glass Tour — £65pp

The signature experience at Leonardslee Lakes & Gardens, pairing the historic gardens with the wine. The Gardens themselves are open to the public seven days a week.

Dogs

Welcome across most of the estate

Dogs are welcome across most parts of the estate, with two exceptions: Leonardslee House and Restaurant Interlude.

Getting there

Straightforward from London — around 45 minutes direct from Victoria or London Bridge, then a short 10-minute taxi to either property.

Staying the night

Leonardslee House — Grade II-listed, tucked inside the Gardens, 10 rooms, steps from the award-winning Restaurant Interlude. The Vineyard Hotel — just across the road in the woodland of the Wine Estate, 9 rooms looking out over the vines.

Common questions

What wine experiences does Leonardslee offer, and what do they cost?

Two signature experiences: the Brunch, Tour and Tasting at Leonardslee Wine Estate at £49.50 per person, and the Gardens to Glass Tour at Leonardslee Lakes and Gardens at £65 per person.

Is Leonardslee dog-friendly?

Dogs are welcome across most parts of the estate. The two exceptions are Leonardslee House and Restaurant Interlude.

Are the Leonardslee gardens open to the public?

Yes — the Gardens are open to the public seven days a week.

How do you get to Leonardslee from London?

It is around 45 minutes direct from London Victoria or London Bridge, then a short 10-minute taxi to either property.

Can you stay at Leonardslee?

Yes, at two properties. Leonardslee House is Grade II-listed with 10 rooms inside the Gardens, steps from Restaurant Interlude. The Vineyard Hotel sits across the road in the woodland of the Wine Estate with 9 rooms overlooking the vines.

What should a first-time visitor not miss?

The estate’s own pick is the Brunch, Tour and Tasting — the vineyards are surrounded by forest, and you drive your own electric golf buggy out to them.
