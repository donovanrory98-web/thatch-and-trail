Quoins Organic Vineyard

Little Ashley, Bradford-on-Avon · Wiltshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

A small organic vineyard and orchard near Bradford-on-Avon with two-hour group tours in July and August, picnics from Hartley Farm, and children and dogs welcome.

The visit

Quoins runs tours for groups of ten or more in July and August; smaller parties can sometimes join a booked group.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours

July and August only.

Confirmed by the estate

Group size

A minimum of 10. A couple or small family can sometimes join an existing booking, with everyone’s permission; the vineyard arranges it.

Confirmed by the estate

Picnics

Hartley Farm delivers picnics; groups arrange this themselves.

Confirmed by the estate

Children and dogs

Both welcome.

Getting there

It’s too far to walk from Bradford-on-Avon; take a taxi from just outside the station.

Common questions

When are Quoins’ tours?

In July and August only, for groups of 10 or more.

Can a couple visit Quoins?

Sometimes: smaller parties can join an existing booking with everyone’s permission.

Are dogs welcome at Quoins?

Yes, children and dogs are both fine.
