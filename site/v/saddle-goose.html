Vineyards / The South West / Saddle Goose

Somerset · South West

Saddle Goose

Frome · Somerset

Estate-verified · November 2026

An urban winery and cellar door in Frome, 30 seconds from the station, open on Saturdays into the evening, with tastings by appointment and a busy events calendar planned for 2027.

The winery bar in Frome · Photograph courtesy of Saddle Goose

The visit

Saddle Goose is right by Frome station; Saturday is the day to drop in, and fuller tastings can be arranged most days.

Confirmed by the estate

Saturdays

Saturdays 12 to 9pm, with a 2pm tasting of four wines (£10, as confirmed November 2026) when booked online. Expected to carry on next year.

Confirmed by the estate

Winter

Closed over Christmas and in January (like most of Frome), reopening from mid to late February. Check the website or socials for the date.

Confirmed by the estate

Tastings by appointment

A fuller tasting of six wines with local cheese and charcuterie (£30 per person, as confirmed November 2026), on most days including winter, depending on availability. Buy it as a voucher on the website or just email.

What’s on

Events in 2027

Bookable events every week or two: winemaker tastings, food and wine nights, live music or DJs, and wine education evenings. Dates go on the website.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs, children and access

Dogs and children are welcome, and the winery is good for disabled access.

Getting there

It’s about 30 seconds’ walk from Frome station.

Common questions

When is Saddle Goose open?

On Saturdays, 12 to 9pm. It closes over Christmas and in January and reopens from mid to late February.

Can you get to Saddle Goose by train?

Yes, it’s about 30 seconds from Frome station.

Are dogs allowed at Saddle Goose?

Yes, dogs and children are welcome.
