Vineyards / Hampshire / Black Chalk

Hampshire

Black Chalk

near Andover · Hampshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A sparkling wine estate in the Test Valley, open seven days a week, with winter tours and lunch, summer public tours, Friday Night Lates and treehouse tastings.

The visit

Black Chalk is open every day of the week all year, so it’s one of the easiest Hampshire estates to plan around.

Confirmed by the estate

January and February

Open seven days a week. The Winter Tour + Tasting (£35, with three wines and lunch) runs Friday to Sunday at 11am. Private tours (£50, four wines) run Tuesday to Thursday at 11am and 2pm.

Confirmed by the estate

March to September

Open seven days a week. Public tours run Friday to Sunday: 11am with lunch (£45) or 2pm without (£30), each with three wines. A midweek tour with lunch (£35) runs on Thursdays at 11am, and private tours on Tuesdays and Wednesdays at 11am.

Confirmed by the estate

Treehouse tastings

Wild Escape Treehouse Tastings run at 2pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays: £120 for two, £20 for each extra guest.

What’s on

Friday Night Lates

Street food and wine every Friday all year, except over Christmas and New Year and the first two Fridays of January.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

Black Chalk’s first pick is the Wild Escapes treehouses. It also suggests The Grosvenor in Stockbridge and The Great Decoy in Hurstbourne Tarrant.

Confirmed by the estate

Don’t miss

The estate’s tip for first-timers: join the vineyard and winery tour, the best way behind the scenes.

Common questions

Is Black Chalk open in winter?

Yes, seven days a week, with a Winter Tour + Tasting with lunch Friday to Sunday at 11am.

What are Black Chalk’s Friday Night Lates?

Street food and wine every Friday, all year apart from Christmas, New Year and the first two Fridays of January.

Where should you stay near Black Chalk?

The estate suggests the Wild Escapes treehouses first, then The Grosvenor in Stockbridge or The Great Decoy in Hurstbourne Tarrant.
