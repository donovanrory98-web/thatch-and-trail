The Wealden Wine Trail

Winklestone Vineyard

Ashdown Forest / Uckfield · East Sussex

Estate-verified · October 2026

A vineyard by Ashdown Forest, open for tours and tastings from May to September, with homemade nibbles and dogs welcome. Groups of ten or more can book all year.

The visit

Winklestone is a summer visit for most people, May to September, and it’s an easy one to bring the dog to.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours and tastings, May to September

Winklestone is open for tours and tastings from May to September. Larger groups (10 or more) and corporate bookings can be arranged all year round.

Food

Homemade nibbles

Tours and tastings come with a selection of homemade nibbles.

Dogs

Dogs welcome

Dogs are welcome on site.

Getting there

Without a car, the estate suggests Buxted station and then a short taxi ride, or Uckfield station and a short ride on the Brighton & Hove 29 bus towards Tunbridge Wells.

Common questions

When can you visit Winklestone Vineyard?

Tours and tastings run from May to September. Groups of ten or more, and corporate bookings, can be arranged all year.

Are dogs welcome at Winklestone?

Yes, dogs are welcome on site.

Is there food at Winklestone?

Tours and tastings come with homemade nibbles.

How do you get to Winklestone without a car?

Buxted station and a short taxi, or Uckfield station and the Brighton & Hove 29 bus towards Tunbridge Wells.
