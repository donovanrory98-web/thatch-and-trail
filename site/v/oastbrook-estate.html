The Rother Wine Triangle

Oastbrook Estate

Robertsbridge · East Sussex

Estate-verified · August 2026

Drop in for a tasting all summer, book a tour by appointment, or stay over with a tour and tasting package. Robertsbridge station is a short taxi ride away.

The visit

Oastbrook works for a spur-of-the-moment visit: tastings are drop-in through the summer, and you only need to book ahead for a tour or a stay.

Confirmed by the estate

Open every day except Monday and Tuesday in summer

Through the summer months Oastbrook is open every day except Mondays and Tuesdays. Tastings are drop-in, or you can book one; tours are by appointment.

Winter

Saturday winery tours, November to spring

Once the summer season ends, winery tours run on Saturdays through to spring.

Staying over

Packages with a tour and tasting

Stays can be booked as a package with a tour and tasting. Prices depend on the time of year: the estate quotes the Hobbit House from £700 for two nights and Avalon from £500.

Getting there

Take the train to Robertsbridge and a taxi from the station. If you drive, there’s parking on site.

Make a day of it

Bodiam Castle and the steam railway

The estate says Bodiam Castle and the heritage railway can both be done in a day with a visit. On Sundays from April to October, the Rother Triangle Bus Tour links the local vineyards.

The estate’s own tip

Don’t miss the sunrise

Asked what a first-time visitor shouldn’t miss, the estate’s answer was the wines, or sunrise over the vines.

Common questions

When is Oastbrook Estate open?

In the summer months Oastbrook is open every day except Monday and Tuesday. From November through to spring, winery tours run on Saturdays.

Do you need to book a tasting at Oastbrook?

No. Tastings are drop-in, though you can also book one. Tours are by appointment.

Can you stay at Oastbrook Estate?

Yes. Stays can be booked as a package with a tour and tasting, and prices depend on the time of year. The estate quotes the Hobbit House from £700 for two nights and Avalon from £500.

How do you get to Oastbrook Estate without a car?

Take the train to Robertsbridge and a taxi from the station. There is parking on site if you drive.

Can you visit Oastbrook and Bodiam Castle in one day?

Yes. The estate says Bodiam Castle and the heritage railway can both be done in a day alongside a visit.

What is the Rother Triangle Bus Tour?

A tour bus linking the Rother vineyards, running every Sunday from April to October. Oastbrook recommends it.
