Yorkshire

Dunesforde

Upper Dunsforth, near York · Yorkshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A North Yorkshire vineyard near York with a summer terrace and wine bar on Friday and Saturday evenings, tours twice a day, and Christmas fairs.

The visit

Dunesforde is a Friday-and-Saturday visit from April to September, with Christmas fairs in late November.

Confirmed by the estate

Terrace and Wine Bar

Fridays and Saturdays, 12 to 8pm, generally from April to the end of September.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours

The Still Wine Experience and the Classic Experience run on Fridays and Saturdays at 12pm and 3pm.

What’s on

Christmas Fairs

Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026, 10am to 4pm.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

Both welcome, as long as they’re well behaved.

Getting there

Cattal is the closest station, about a 10-minute taxi away. There’s no taxi rank, so book one ahead.

Common questions

When is Dunesforde’s wine bar open?

Fridays and Saturdays, 12 to 8pm, generally from April to the end of September.

Are dogs welcome at Dunesforde?

Yes, and children too, as long as they’re well behaved.

How do you get to Dunesforde without a car?

Train to Cattal, then a pre-booked 10-minute taxi.
