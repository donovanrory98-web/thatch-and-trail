Chartham Vineyard

Chartham / Canterbury · Kent

Estate-verified · October 2026

An owner-run vineyard three minutes’ walk from Chartham station near Canterbury, with Saturday tours and tastings, wine by the glass and picnics in the old farmyard.

The visit

Chartham is probably the easiest vineyard in Kent to reach by train: it’s a three-minute walk from Chartham station.

Confirmed by the estate

Three minutes from the station

The vineyard is next to Chartham station, a 3-minute walk from the platform. Chartham is the first stop from Canterbury West (6 minutes), and there’s a bus stop opposite with regular services from Canterbury.

Confirmed by the estate

Bring a picnic

No food on site, but the village shop is about 3 minutes’ walk away and picnics are encouraged in the historic farmyard. Still and sparkling wine by the glass or bottle.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

Plenty of places in Canterbury and in local farmhouses.

From their website

Saturday tours and shop

Guided tours with a tutored tasting on Saturdays at 11am and 2pm, led by the owners; book ahead. The shop opens Saturdays, 10.30am to 5pm. Well-behaved dogs on leads are welcome.

Getting there

Take the train to Chartham, the first stop from Canterbury West, then walk 3 minutes. Buses from Canterbury stop opposite.

Common questions

Can you get to Chartham Vineyard by train?

Yes, it’s a 3-minute walk from Chartham station, the first stop from Canterbury West.

Is there food at Chartham Vineyard?

No, but the village shop is 3 minutes away and picnics are welcome in the farmyard. Wine is sold by the glass or bottle.

When are the tours at Chartham Vineyard?

Saturdays at 11am and 2pm, booked in advance.

Are dogs welcome at Chartham Vineyard?

Yes, well-behaved dogs on leads.
