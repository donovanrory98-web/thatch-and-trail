Horsham & the Arun Valley

Kinsbrook Vineyard

Pulborough · West Sussex

Estate-verified · August 2026

Run by two of the country’s youngest vineyard owners, with a farm shop, restaurant and its own winery on site — daily 11am tours at £30, dogs welcome everywhere, and under-18s allowed along too.

The visit

Kinsbrook is one of the few Sussex estates where a tour works for the whole party: dogs are welcome throughout, under-18s can join the tour, and the walking route now takes in the new on-site winery as well as the vines.

Confirmed by the estate

Public tours £30pp, private £40pp

The public tour and tasting is £30 per person, running daily at 11am and lasting around 90 minutes — a walking tour plus a tasting of three KIN wines with nibbles. Private tours are £40 per person and can be arranged at any time, on any day.

Confirmed by the estate

The tour covers the winery and the vines

Since the on-site winery opened, the route takes in both the winery and the vineyard — not just a walk through the rows.

Families

Under-18s are welcome on the tour

Children can join tours and tastings, though they can’t take part in the tasting itself. The estate offers alternatives — non-alcoholic sparkling or a soft drink.

Dogs

Welcome everywhere

Well-behaved dogs are welcome throughout Kinsbrook — patio, restaurant and tours. The estate asks that they’re kept on a lead where appropriate.

Getting there — which station

Billingshurst is the closest, then Pulborough, then Horsham. If you haven’t pre-booked, Horsham is your best bet for finding a taxi on the spot — all three have reliable services you can book ahead, and the estate keeps a list of local firms.

Finding it — worth reading

Sat navs don’t always register the address. Use RH20 2LU, which is the postcode the estate uses — RH20 2RZ is their postal address and will send you to a different spot. what3words: ///widely.reacting.cubed. There’s ample free parking on site.

The estate’s own tip

A private tasting with dinner, Friday or Saturday

The estate’s pick is a private wine tasting paired with dinner on a Friday or Saturday evening — for the view from the farmhouse balcony at golden hour. Head Chef Martin Price cooks with produce grown on the estate and from local suppliers. In summer, the two-day Summer Festival of live music is the other one not to miss.

Dates for the diary

Christmas Market and winter workshops

The annual Christmas Market is on 5–6 December. (There won’t be a Harvest Festival again, the estate tells us.) Through the winter the estate runs workshops paired with wine tastings — candle painting, and wreath-making closer to Christmas — and the restaurant stays open Thursday to Sunday, with Sunday roasts taking centre stage.

Common questions

How much is a tour at Kinsbrook Vineyard?

The public tour and tasting is £30 per person, running daily at 11am for around 90 minutes and including a tasting of three KIN wines with nibbles. Private tours are £40 per person and can be arranged any day, at any time.

Is Kinsbrook Vineyard dog-friendly?

Yes — well-behaved dogs are welcome everywhere at Kinsbrook, including the patio, the restaurant and on tours. The estate asks that dogs are kept on a lead where appropriate.

Can children go on a tour at Kinsbrook?

Yes. Under-18s are welcome to join tours and tastings, though they cannot take part in the tasting itself. The estate provides alternatives such as non-alcoholic sparkling or a soft drink.

Which station is closest to Kinsbrook Vineyard?

Billingshurst is the closest, followed by Pulborough and then Horsham. Horsham is the best bet for finding a taxi on the spot, but all three have reliable services that can be booked in advance.

What postcode should I use for Kinsbrook Vineyard?

Does the Kinsbrook tour include the winery?

Yes — since the on-site winery opened, the tour takes in both the winery and the vines.

What is on at Kinsbrook in winter?

The estate runs workshops paired with wine tastings, including candle painting and wreath-making closer to Christmas. The Christmas Market runs 5–6 December, and the restaurant stays open Thursday to Sunday with Sunday roasts.
