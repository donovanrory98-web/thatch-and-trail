Vineyards / The South West / Aldwick Estate

Somerset · South West

Aldwick Estate

Redhill, near Bristol · Somerset

Estate-verified · November 2026

An estate at Redhill, near Bristol, with vineyard tours on Mondays and Sundays from April to October and the Mendip Christmas Fair in its barns each November.

The visit

Aldwick is a spring-to-autumn tour visit; outside that, you can call in for wine during office hours.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours, April to October

Tours generally run on Mondays at 12pm and Sundays at 12pm and 2:30pm, subject to other events. April dates are on the website, with the rest of 2027 to follow.

Confirmed by the estate

Buying wine

Wine is sold online, and visitors are welcome to drop in during office hours, Monday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm, as long as a private event isn’t on. Ring ahead if in doubt.

What’s on

Mendip Christmas Fair

Held in the estate’s barns most years; this year it’s on 14 and 15 November.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Assistance dogs only on tours and tastings.

Common questions

When are Aldwick Estate’s tours?

From April to October, generally Mondays at 12pm and Sundays at 12pm and 2:30pm.

Can you buy wine at Aldwick in person?

Yes, Monday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm, unless a private event is on. Ring ahead if in doubt.

Are dogs allowed on Aldwick tours?

Only assistance dogs.
