Horsham & the Arun Valley

Nyetimber

West Chiltington · West Sussex

Estate-verified · August 2026

England’s most famous sparkling wine house opens to the public only on a handful of Open Days each year — and the 2026 tours have already sold out.

The visit

Nyetimber is not a drop-in vineyard. There is no cellar door, no tasting room and no self-guided trail. Public access is limited to a small number of Open Day Tours, and if you want to go, the planning starts a year ahead.

Confirmed by the estate · August 2026

2026 Open Day Tours are sold out

Open Day Tours run at £50 per person, and the 2026 dates are now fully sold out. The estate occasionally receives cancellations and keeps a waiting list — email their customer service team to be added.

Planning for next year

2027 dates announced in Q1

The 2027 Open Day Tours will be announced in the first quarter of 2027, with the exact date still to be confirmed. The estate recommends signing up to their newsletter, which is where upcoming experiences and events are announced first.

If you want to go

Join the newsletter now and watch for the Q1 2027 announcement. These sell out, so treat the release date as the thing to diarise rather than the tour date.

Common questions

Can you visit Nyetimber?

Only on their Open Day Tours, which run on a small number of dates each year at £50 per person. There is no cellar door, tasting room or self-guided trail.

Are Nyetimber Open Day Tours available in 2026?

No — the 2026 Open Day Tours are fully sold out. The estate occasionally receives cancellations and maintains a waiting list, which their customer service team can add you to.

When are Nyetimber 2027 tours announced?

The 2027 Open Day Tours will be announced in the first quarter of 2027, with the date to be confirmed. Signing up to the estate newsletter is the way to hear first.

How much does a Nyetimber tour cost?

Open Day Tours are £50 per person.
