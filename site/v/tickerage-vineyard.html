The Wealden Wine Trail

Tickerage Vineyard

Blackboys · East Sussex

Estate-verified · November 2026

A vineyard near Blackboys in East Sussex with open-day tastings a few times a year, private tasting experiences, and Christmas Sips in December.

The visit

Tickerage’s open days come round a few times a year, so watch for dates; private tastings are booked through the website.

Confirmed by the estate

Open days

The dates vary, but there’s normally one at Easter, a couple in summer and another at Christmas. Prices are on the website.

What’s on

Christmas Sips

Saturday 5 December, from 12:30pm, with last pour at 2:30pm. Open to anyone over 18, with limited tickets.

From their website

Private tastings

As well as the open days, you can book a private tasting. See the website for what’s included.

Getting there

Uckfield station is about 3 miles away. There’s one bus an hour to Blackboys, then a 5-minute walk. Taxis are rare, so plan the trip back.

Common questions

When are Tickerage’s open days?

They vary, but there’s normally one at Easter, a couple in summer and one at Christmas.

How do you get to Tickerage without a car?

Train to Uckfield, then the hourly bus to Blackboys and a 5-minute walk. Taxis are rare.

Is Christmas Sips at Tickerage for adults only?

Yes, it’s open to anyone over 18, with limited tickets.
