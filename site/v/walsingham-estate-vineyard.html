Walsingham Estate Vineyard

Walsingham · Norfolk

Estate-verified · November 2026

A North Norfolk vineyard in the Stiffkey Valley whose Crypt Tastings run in April and on summer evenings from July to September, with vineyard tours by appointment and the full range in the village farm shop.

The visit

Walsingham’s 2027 Crypt Tastings are already on sale; outside those, tours are by appointment.

Confirmed by the estate

Crypt Tastings in 2027

On sale now through Eventbrite and the website. April (4pm): Sat 3, Sun 4. July (5pm): Sat 17, Thu 22, Sat 24, Thu 29. August (5pm): Thu 5, Sat 7, Thu 12, Sat 14, Thu 19, Sat 21. September (5pm): Thu 9, Sat 11.

Common questions

When are the Walsingham Crypt Tastings in 2027?

3 and 4 April at 4pm, then Thursday and Saturday evenings at 5pm from 17 July to 11 September.

Can you tour Walsingham Estate Vineyard?

Yes, by appointment.

Where can you buy Walsingham wine in the village?

Walsingham Farms Shop on Guild Street stocks the full range.
