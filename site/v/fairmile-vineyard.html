Fairmile Vineyard

Henley-on-Thames · Oxfordshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

A dog- and child-friendly vineyard on the Fairmile above Henley, with tours by appointment every day of the year and a 30-minute walk from the station along what William Morris called “the loveliest approach to any town in England”.

Raising a glass on the slope at Fairmile · Photograph courtesy of Jan Mirkowski, Fairmile Vineyard

The visit

Fairmile is open all year: tours by appointment any day, and a short cellar door window three days a week. It is one of the few vineyards you can easily walk to from a station.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours by appointment

Every day of the year. The estate prefers a day or two’s notice, but if a tour is already running (especially on summer weekends) they can sometimes fit you in at much shorter notice.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door

Tuesdays and Saturdays 10am to noon, and Thursdays noon to 2pm, with no change planned for next year.

From their website

On the tour

About an hour through the vines, ending with a tutored tasting of the sparkling Rosé and Classic Cuvée on a slope overlooking the Chilterns, where red kites circle below.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

The vineyard is fully deer- and rabbit-fenced, so it is dog- and child-friendly.

Confirmed by the estate

Regatta week

The vineyard stays open, but driving through Henley isn’t advisable. Come from the Bix, Nettlebed or Oxford (northern) side, or walk up from town. Henley Lions Club normally runs all-day parking on the Fairmile’s grass verges that week.

Getting there

From Henley-on-Thames station it is about a 30-minute walk through the town or alongside the Regatta course. William Morris called the Fairmile “the loveliest approach to any town in England”.

Common questions

Can you walk to Fairmile Vineyard from Henley station?

Yes, it is about a 30-minute walk through the town or alongside the Regatta course.

How much notice does Fairmile need for a tour?

A day or two is preferred, though they can sometimes add visitors to a tour that is already running.

Are dogs allowed at Fairmile Vineyard?

Yes. The vineyard is fully deer- and rabbit-fenced, so it is dog- and child-friendly.

Is Fairmile open during Henley Regatta?

Yes. Avoid driving through town and approach from the Bix, Nettlebed or Oxford side, or walk up from Henley.
