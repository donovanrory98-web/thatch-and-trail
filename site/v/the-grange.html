Vineyards / Hampshire / The Grange

Hampshire

The Grange

Itchen Stoke, near Alresford · Hampshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

A family estate above the River Itchen with two-hour vineyard and winery tours from February to December, a cellar door six days a week, holiday cottages and summer Thursday Lates.

The vineyard at The Grange at sunset · Photograph courtesy of The Grange

The visit

The Grange is open for most of the year: tours run from February to December, the cellar door is open six days a week, and you can stay on the estate.

Confirmed by the estate

Vineyard Tour & Tasting

11am to 1pm, no more than 15 people, through Burges Field and the 2022 winery and on to the tasting room. Running February to December 2027; dates and tickets on The Grange’s tours page.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door

A small shop at the winery, open to anyone: Monday to Friday 10am to 4pm and Saturday 11am to 4pm.

Confirmed by the estate

Thursday Lates

From May to September the winery opens on Thursday evenings, pouring wine by the glass (magnums included) with a different local food hero each week. The estate’s pick for a first visit.

Confirmed by the estate

Staying on the estate

Holiday cottages on the estate: Abbotstone Farm House, Lake House Cottage, Garden Cottage and the Clock Tower, bookable through The Grange’s estate website.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

Both are welcome on the tours.

Getting there

Take the train to Winchester; the vineyard and winery are about 15 minutes by taxi.

Visit The Grange’s website →

Common questions

When do The Grange’s vineyard tours run?

From February to December 2027, 11am to 1pm, for up to 15 people. Book through The Grange’s tours page.

Can I visit The Grange’s cellar door without a tour?

Yes. It is open to anyone, Monday to Friday 10am to 4pm and Saturday 11am to 4pm.

Can you stay at The Grange?

Yes: Abbotstone Farm House, Lake House Cottage, Garden Cottage and the Clock Tower are holiday cottages on the estate.

Are dogs and children allowed on The Grange tours?

Yes, both are welcome.
