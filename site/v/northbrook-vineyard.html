Vineyards / Hampshire / Northbrook Vineyard

Hampshire

Northbrook Vineyard

Bishop’s Waltham · Hampshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Hampshire vineyard near Bishop’s Waltham with a Christmas cellar door, online orders to collect, and tours, tastings and events planned for 2027.

The visit

Northbrook isn’t open to the public day to day yet; you can order online and collect, and tours and tastings are planned for next year.

Confirmed by the estate

Visiting now

Not open to the public at the moment. Order online and pick up from the cellar door.

What’s on

Christmas Cellar Door

Dates and times are on the Almanac page of Northbrook’s website.

Confirmed by the estate

Next year

The plan is tours and tastings and a programme of bookable events. Details will go on Instagram too.

Confirmed by the estate

Finding it

Postcode SO32 1FB; what3words ///organic.recount.reckons takes you straight to the cellar door.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Large areas of the estate have been taken out of production for wildlife, so Northbrook would prefer visitors not to bring dogs.

Getting there

The nearest station is Botley.

Common questions

Can you visit Northbrook Vineyard?

Not day to day yet. You can order online and collect, and visit the Christmas Cellar Door; tours and tastings are planned for 2027.

Where is Northbrook Vineyard?

Beeches Hill, Bishop’s Waltham, SO32 1FB (what3words ///organic.recount.reckons).

Can you bring a dog to Northbrook?

The estate would prefer not, to protect the wildlife habitats on the estate.
