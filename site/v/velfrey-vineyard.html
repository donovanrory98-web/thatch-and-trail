Velfrey Vineyard

Lampeter Velfrey, near Narberth · Pembrokeshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Pembrokeshire vineyard near Narberth with 90-minute tours twice a week, guided tastings all year, sharing boards, and a welcome for children and dogs.

The visit

Velfrey runs tours and tastings all year round, all starting at 11am.

Confirmed by the estate

Visitor Centre

Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 12:30pm, and Saturday 10am to 2pm. Closed Mondays and Sundays.

Confirmed by the estate

Vineyard Tours

Thursdays and Saturdays at 11am, about 90 minutes. A sharing board can be added on Saturdays, with 48 hours’ notice.

Confirmed by the estate

Guided wine tastings

About 45 minutes, at 11am: Tuesdays and Fridays from April to October, and Thursdays and Saturdays from November to March.

Confirmed by the estate

Children and dogs

Children are welcome, and Velfrey is dog friendly.

Getting there

Whitland is the nearest station. There are no taxis waiting, so pre-book one, or it’s about a 30-minute walk.

Common questions

When are Velfrey’s tours?

Thursdays and Saturdays at 11am, for about 90 minutes.

Does Velfrey do tastings in winter?

Yes, on Thursdays and Saturdays at 11am from November to March.

Is Velfrey dog friendly?

Yes, and children are welcome too.
