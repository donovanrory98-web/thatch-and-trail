Helme Edge Vineyard

Meltham, near Huddersfield · Yorkshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

A vineyard at Meltham, near Huddersfield, with tastings three times a day, Monday to Saturday, including a charcuterie tasting and a Yorkshire wine and cheese tasting.

The visit

Helme Edge runs tastings six days a week, all year, and it’s easy to reach by bus or taxi from Huddersfield.

Confirmed by the estate

Tastings, Monday to Saturday

11am Charcuterie Tasting, 2pm Standard Tasting, and 4pm Standard or Wine and Cheese.

Confirmed by the estate

Wine and Cheese

Expected to run through 2027.

Confirmed by the estate

Parking

Park on site (car-share if you can). Or park near Blackmoorfoot and walk 5 to 10 minutes.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Readily welcomed. While food is served, dogs may be asked to wait outside for a short while.

Confirmed by the estate

Children

Licensing is tight, so it isn’t really set up for children.

Getting there

Take the 324 bus from Huddersfield to Meltham Broadlands, then a 10-minute walk. Uber is reliable locally, and Bridge Taxis know the vineyard well.

Common questions

When are Helme Edge’s tastings?

Monday to Saturday at 11am (charcuterie), 2pm and 4pm.

Can you get to Helme Edge by bus?

Yes, the 324 from Huddersfield to Meltham Broadlands, then a 10-minute walk.

Are dogs welcome at Helme Edge?

Yes, though they may be asked to wait outside briefly while food is served.
