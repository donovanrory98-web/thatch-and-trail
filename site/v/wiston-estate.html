Worthing & the Downs

Wiston Estate

Washington · West Sussex

Estate-verified · July 2026

Two hundred and seventy-five years of the same family on the same chalk, one of England's most decorated winemakers, and a wood-fired restaurant to make an afternoon of it. A proper Sussex wine day.

Twenty years on from the first plantings · Photograph supplied by Wiston Estate

The visit

Wiston works as a booked tour and tasting or as a relaxed drop-in for a glass and a walk, wrapped around the Chalk restaurant and 58km of open footpaths.

Tours & tastings

Best booked ahead

Guided tours and tastings run on set days and times, so they are best booked in advance. The standard tour and tasting is £30 per person.

No tour required

Cellar Door & Wine Flights

Prefer to just taste? Counter tastings in the Cellar Door are £10 per person — or free if you buy a bottle. Wine Flights, in NV and vintage options, are served in the courtyard at Chalk.

Free to explore

58km of footpaths

The estate has 58km of public footpaths and bridleways, free to walk without booking a thing. Dogs are welcome on leads, and so are families and picnics.

Getting there

Nearest stations are Worthing and Horsham, both with regular services from London and Brighton, and both about a 20-minute taxi or Uber to the estate. There is free parking at the winery.

New for 2026

Wiston is marking 20 years since its first vines with two new releases: Tank Five Chardonnay, its first still Chardonnay from 100% estate fruit, and Fifty Summers Rosé. A big awards year too — the Vintage Cuvée Magnum 2009 took the English Sparkling Trophy at 97 points, and Wiston was named WineGB's Best Contract Winery.

Common questions

Do you need to book a Wiston tour, and what does it cost?

Tours and tastings run on set days and times and are best booked ahead. The standard tour and tasting is £30 per person. For just a taste, Cellar Door counter tastings are £10 per person, or free if you buy a bottle.

Can you visit Wiston without booking a tour?

Yes. You can drop in for a Cellar Door counter tasting or a Wine Flight in the Chalk courtyard, eat at the restaurant, and walk the estate footpaths — all without a booked tour.

Are dogs welcome at Wiston?

Dogs are welcome in the courtyard, on the Vineyard Explorer tour (on a lead) and on the estate's public footpaths. They cannot join the winery tour or come inside the Chalk restaurant.

Can families and children visit?

Yes. Families and under-18s are welcome at Chalk, on the estate walks and for picnics. Under-18s cannot take part in the winery tours, tastings or other events where alcohol is served.

Can you walk the estate for free?

Yes. Wiston has 58km of public footpaths and bridleways, open to walk without booking a tour.

What's the nearest station to Wiston?

Worthing or Horsham — both have regular trains from London and Brighton and are about a 20-minute taxi from the estate. There is free parking if you drive.

What should a first-time visitor not miss?

Walk through the vines before or after your visit, then settle into Chalk for lunch or a glass in the courtyard — the best way to feel the chalk landscape that shapes every bottle.
