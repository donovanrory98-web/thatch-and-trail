ARK Wines at Mount Farm Vineyards

near Sudbury · Suffolk

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Suffolk vineyard near Sudbury open Wednesday to Saturday, with Sunday vineyard lunches, a monthly Stitch & Wine Club, ARK Sessions music nights, and dogs welcome in the winery.

The visit

ARK is open four days a week, with Sundays kept for its vineyard lunches and special events.

Confirmed by the estate

Opening days

Wednesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm, and on Sundays for vineyard lunches and occasional special events.

Confirmed by the estate

Sunday vineyard lunches

Running every week and planned to carry on into next year, with the odd break. Book through the website.

What’s on

Stitch & Wine and ARK Sessions

The Stitch & Wine Club meets on the last Wednesday of the month, for the rest of this year at least. ARK Sessions are bi-monthly music and social evenings.

What’s on

Harvest Party

Saturday 21 November. See ARK’s website for times and tickets.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Very welcome in the winery, as long as they’re friendly and comfortable with other dogs: the vineyard has a few of its own.

Getting there

Sudbury is the closest station, about 20 minutes by taxi; book ahead, especially in the evening. Bury St Edmunds is about 30 minutes, with better taxi availability, and Colchester about 40 minutes, handy from London.

Common questions

When is ARK Wines open?

Wednesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm, plus Sundays for vineyard lunches and events.

Are dogs welcome at ARK Wines?

Yes, very welcome in the winery, if friendly and well behaved.

How do you get to ARK Wines by train?

Sudbury station, then a pre-booked taxi of about 20 minutes. Bury St Edmunds and Colchester also work.
