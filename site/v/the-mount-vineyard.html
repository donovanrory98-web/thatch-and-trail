Kent · West Kent

The Mount Vineyard

Shoreham / Sevenoaks · Kent

A vineyard in Shoreham village near Sevenoaks with a courtyard bar and restaurant, weekend tastings all year, and a 10-minute walk from the station.

The visit

From The Mount Vineyard’s own website, October 2026. We’re checking these details with the estate, so confirm before you travel.

Tastings

Friday to Sunday

Tastings run Friday to Sunday at 12.30pm and 2.30pm, all year. From late May to September there’s an evening tasting with a vineyard tour. Tastings are adults only.

Food

The Courtyard Bar and Restaurant

The Courtyard Bar and Restaurant is open Wednesday to Saturday from 12pm (kitchen until 9pm) and Sunday 12pm to 6pm. Only food and drink bought on site can be consumed there.

The shop

Ten Acres Shop

The Ten Acres Shop serves coffee and pastries on Saturday and Sunday mornings.

Getting there

Shoreham station is about a 10-minute walk. The vineyard recommends coming on foot or by public transport.

Common questions

When are tastings at The Mount Vineyard?

Friday to Sunday at 12.30pm and 2.30pm, all year. Adults only.

Is there a restaurant at The Mount Vineyard?

Yes, the Courtyard Bar and Restaurant, open Wednesday to Sunday.

Can you walk to The Mount Vineyard from the station?

Yes. Shoreham station is about a 10-minute walk.
