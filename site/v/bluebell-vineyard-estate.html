The Wealden Wine Trail

Bluebell Vineyard Estate

Furners Green / Uckfield · East Sussex

100,000+ vines on edge of Ashdown Forest. Three woodland/vineyard trails, famous for spring bluebells. Tours from £25.

The visit

Open to visitors for tours and tastings. Shop Tue–Sat; tours pre-book.

Getting there

Nearest station: Haywards Heath/Uckfield (20 min). Sussex vineyards sit down winding lanes — check parking and access with the estate if you're not driving.
