Dorset · South West

Bride Valley Vineyard

Litton Cheney, near Dorchester · Dorset

Estate-verified · November 2026

A Dorset vineyard in the Bride Valley, with Saturday morning tours from mid-April, summer vineyard picnics, and lakeside camping within walking distance.

The visit

Bride Valley is a Saturday-morning visit from mid-April to September; bring a picnic, as there’s no food on site.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours

Saturdays at 10:30am, from mid-April to the end of September.

What’s on

Vineyard picnics

Planned for the last Friday of the month, June to September; check the website nearer the time.

Confirmed by the estate

Food

No food on sale, but you’re welcome to bring a picnic to enjoy in the vineyard.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

Litton Lakes: lakeside camping with paddleboarding and a café, walking distance from the vineyard. Or The Outbuildings, a boutique farm hotel and restaurant near Bridport, about 15 minutes’ drive.

Getting there

The nearest stations are Dorchester and Maiden Newton; it’s about a 15-minute taxi to the vineyard.

Common questions

When can you tour Bride Valley?

On Saturdays at 10:30am, from mid-April to the end of September.

Can you bring a picnic to Bride Valley?

Yes. There’s no food on sale, but picnics are welcome in the vineyard.

Where can you stay near Bride Valley?

Litton Lakes is within walking distance; The Outbuildings near Bridport is about 15 minutes’ drive.
