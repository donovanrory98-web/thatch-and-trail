Sussex vineyards / Horsham & the Arun Valley / Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards)

Horsham & the Arun Valley

Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards)

Pulborough · West Sussex

Estate-verified · October 2026

Award-winning English sparkling wine, open for tastings and tours almost every day of the year, with private tours led by the owner or winemaker.

The visit

Ambriel is open throughout the year, almost every day. Pick a date on the booking page and the free times appear in the drop-down.

Confirmed by the estate

Three ways to visit

Choose a Tasting, a Tour and Tasting, or a Private Tour and Tasting with the owner or winemaker. All three carry on next season.

Where you taste

Barn or terrace, depending on the weather

On hot days tastings move out to the terrace; when it’s chilly you’ll be in the tasting barn.

Booking

Availability most days

There’s availability most days. Book online and the free times for your chosen date show in the drop-down.

What’s on

FIZZTIVITIES on 22 November

Ambriel runs events through the year, with first booking for wine club members. Its pre-Christmas FIZZTIVITIES event is on the afternoon of 22 November.

The estate’s own tip

The views

Asked what a first-time visitor shouldn’t miss, the estate’s answer was the spectacular views.

Getting there

Ambriel is on Nutbourne Lane, Pulborough, about halfway between Billingshurst and Pulborough stations. Coming by train from London, the estate suggests getting off at Billingshurst.

Common questions

Is Ambriel open to visitors?

Yes, throughout the year and almost every day, for a tasting, a tour and tasting, or a private tour and tasting with the owner or winemaker.

Do you need to book at Ambriel?

Yes, online. There’s availability most days, and the free times show once you pick a date.

Which station is nearest to Ambriel?

Ambriel sits between Billingshurst and Pulborough stations. From London, the estate suggests Billingshurst.

What events does Ambriel run?

Events through the year, with wine club members booking first. FIZZTIVITIES, a pre-Christmas event, is on the afternoon of 22 November.
