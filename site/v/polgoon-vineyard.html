Cornwall · South West

Polgoon Vineyard

Penzance · Cornwall

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Cornish vineyard at Penzance with guided tours most of the year, a shop open daily through to Christmas, and a summer restaurant; dogs on leads are welcome.

The visit

Polgoon runs tours year round apart from January, with the most choice from June to September, when the restaurant and bar reopen.

Confirmed by the estate

Guided tours in 2027

February to May: Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2pm. June to September: Wednesday to Sunday at 2pm. October to December: Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2pm. No tours in January; they restart at February half-term. (Until Christmas 2026: Wednesdays and Saturdays at 3pm.)

Confirmed by the estate

Shop

Usual hours until Christmas, then 10am to 3pm from 24 December to the end of March, and closed on Sundays over that winter. Closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day.

Confirmed by the estate

Vinehouse restaurant and bar

Closed for the winter; reopening in June and closing again at the end of September.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Dogs on leads are welcome on the guided tours and throughout Polgoon, though not inside the tank room (there’s room outside to hear the guide).

Common questions

When are Polgoon’s tours in 2027?

Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2pm (February to May and October to December), and Wednesday to Sunday at 2pm from June to September. No tours in January.

Is the Vinehouse restaurant open in winter?

No, it reopens in June and closes again at the end of September.

Are dogs allowed at Polgoon?

Yes, on leads, including on the guided tours (but not inside the tank room).
