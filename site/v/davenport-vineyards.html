Davenport Vineyards

Horsmonden · Kent

Estate-verified · November 2026

An organic wine estate at Hazel Street Farm in Horsmonden: a Friday shop, the HoVEC Christmas Market, and monthly tour weekends planned for next year.

The visit

Davenport is a Friday visit for most of the year, with a handful of events; plan around the shop hours, as unarranged visits aren’t encouraged.

Confirmed by the estate

The shop on Fridays

Open Fridays, 11am to 4:30pm, to buy a bottle or a case, or enjoy a glass. Order online with Collection from Winery and they’ll be flexible about a time. Private visits and events can be requested.

What’s on

HoVEC Christmas Market

At Hazel Street Farm on Saturday 5 December, 11am to 3pm, with a minibus from Heath Stores.

Confirmed by the estate

2027 tours

Davenport plans to open one weekend a month for tours and tastings, probably April to August. Dates are due within the month; sign up on its website for updates.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Dogs are allowed in the yard, on a lead, but not on the vineyard walks.

Getting there

It’s about a 15-minute walk from Horsmonden village, partly along a woodland path if you’d rather avoid the road. There’s parking for about 60 cars on site.

Common questions

When can you visit Davenport Vineyards?

The shop is open on Fridays, 11am to 4:30pm. Tour weekends are planned once a month from about April to August 2027.

Can you walk to Davenport from Horsmonden?

Yes, about 15 minutes, partly on a woodland path.

Are dogs allowed at Davenport?

In the yard on a lead, but not on the vineyard walks.
