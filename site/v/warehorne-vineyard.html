Warehorne Vineyard

Warehorne · Kent

A vineyard looking out over Romney Marsh, with a weekend cellar door and a vineyard tour and tasting, a short walk from Ham Street station.

The visit

From Warehorne Vineyard’s own website, October 2026. We’re checking these details with the estate, so confirm before you travel.

Opening

Weekend cellar door

The cellar door and shop are open Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 5pm.

Tours

Vineyard tour and tasting

Book the Vineyard Tour and Wine Tasting Experience; gift vouchers for tours are available too.

The view

Over Romney Marsh

The vineyard sits back from the Warehorne Road with views over Romney Marsh.

Getting there

Ham Street station is a 10 to 15 minute walk; Ashford International is close by for faster trains from London.

Common questions

When is Warehorne Vineyard open?

The cellar door and shop are open Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 5pm.

Can you walk to Warehorne Vineyard from the station?

Yes. Ham Street station is a 10 to 15 minute walk.

Does Warehorne Vineyard do tours?

Yes, a Vineyard Tour and Wine Tasting Experience, booked online.
