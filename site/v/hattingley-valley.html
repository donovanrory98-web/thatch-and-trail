Hampshire

Hattingley Valley

Lower Wield, near Alresford · Hampshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

An award-winning Hampshire sparkling wine producer with behind-the-scenes winery tours and tastings on Saturdays and Sundays, plus Fridays from spring to harvest.

The visit

Hattingley Valley is a winery visit: pre-book a weekend tour (or a Friday from spring to harvest), or call in at the cellar door on a weekday.

Confirmed by the estate

Winery Tour & Tasting

Behind the scenes at the winery on Saturdays and Sundays, and Fridays from spring to harvest. Pre-book on Hattingley Valley’s website to be sure of a place.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door

Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm; Saturdays and Sundays by appointment.

From their website

The wines

A sparkling range including the Classic Reserve NV, a rosé and a Blanc de Blancs, plus a dessert wine, Entice. The winery says it has won over 230 medals.

Common questions

When are Hattingley Valley’s winery tours?

On Saturdays and Sundays, and on Fridays from spring to harvest. Pre-book to be sure of a place.

Can I visit Hattingley Valley without booking a tour?

The cellar door is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. At weekends it is by appointment.
