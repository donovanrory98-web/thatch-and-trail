Kent · The Kent Weald

Woodchurch Wine Estate

Woodchurch · Kent

A vineyard near Woodchurch with a bar and balcony over the vines, open Thursday to Sunday, with a free self-guided tour, guided tours and wine flights.

The visit

From Woodchurch Wine Estate’s own website, October 2026. We’re checking these details with the estate, so confirm before you travel.

Opening

Thursday to Sunday

Open Thursday and Friday 12pm to 5pm and Saturday and Sunday 11am to 5pm. Last food orders are an hour before closing.

Tours

Free walk or a guided tour

Pick up a map at the bar for the free self-guided tour (about 20 minutes), or book the guided tour and tasting (about an hour and a quarter). Wine flights need no booking.

Food

Boards, hot plates and pizza

Snacks, cheese or charcuterie boards and hot plates, with stone-baked pizzas Friday to Sunday. Book ahead to be sure of food.

Getting there

Nearest station: Ashford International, then about 20 minutes by taxi. Footpaths also run from behind Woodchurch church, about half a mile.

Common questions

When is Woodchurch Wine Estate open?

Thursday and Friday 12pm to 5pm, Saturday and Sunday 11am to 5pm.

Can you visit Woodchurch without booking?

Yes, for drinks, wine flights and the free self-guided tour. Book the guided tour, and book ahead to guarantee food.

Is there food at Woodchurch?

Boards, snacks and hot plates, plus stone-baked pizzas from Friday to Sunday.

How do you get to Woodchurch by train?

Ashford International, then about 20 minutes by taxi.
