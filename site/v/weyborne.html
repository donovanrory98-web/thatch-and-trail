Chichester & Petworth

Weyborne

Haslemere · West Sussex

Estate-verified · November 2026

A sparkling wine estate near Haslemere with tours and tastings on one Saturday a month from April, a vineyard tour and picnic through the summer, and the Dovecote and Bathing House to see.

The visit

Weyborne opens for organised tours and tastings on one Saturday a month from April; check the website for the dates.

Confirmed by the estate

Monthly Saturdays

From April, organised tours and tastings run on one Saturday each month. The dates go on the website.

Confirmed by the estate

Vineyard Tour & Picnic

Runs from April to September.

Confirmed by the estate

The Dovecote and the Bathing House

These can be part of your visit.

Getting there

Take the train to Haslemere; the estate says taxis there are good.

Common questions

When can you visit Weyborne?

On one Saturday each month from April for organised tours and tastings, with a tour and picnic from April to September.

How do you get to Weyborne by train?

Go to Haslemere station and take a taxi.

Can you see the Dovecote at Weyborne?

Yes, it can be part of a visit.
