Langham Wine Estate

near Dorchester · Dorset

Estate-verified · October 2026

An award-winning Dorset sparkling wine estate near Dorchester, with a tasting room open five or six days a week, guided tours and lunch at Smiths Kitchen.

The visit

Langham’s core hours are Wednesday to Sunday, with Tuesdays added in summer; check its website for tour dates, which change next year.

Confirmed by the estate

Opening hours

Generally Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, and Tuesday to Sunday from June to September. Hours vary by season, so check the website.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours

Tour dates are changing next year: check the website for details.

Confirmed by the estate

Smiths Kitchen

Lunch from 12pm every day the estate is open. Booking is essential in peak season.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

The estate suggests The Kings Arms in Dorchester, or Aller Dorset for glamping.

Getting there

Take the train to Dorchester, then a taxi.

Common questions

When is Langham Wine Estate open?

Generally Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, and Tuesday to Sunday from June to September.

Can you have lunch at Langham?

Yes, at Smiths Kitchen from 12pm; book in peak season.

How do you get to Langham without a car?

Train to Dorchester, then a taxi.
