Norfolk · East of England

Brecks Vineyard

Hockwold cum Wilton · Norfolk

Estate-verified · November 2026

A vineyard in the Norfolk Brecks with a weekday cellar door, Slow Sundays, last-Friday evenings in the barn, and in-depth tastings every six weeks.

Poppies among the vines · Photograph courtesy of Brecks Vineyard

The visit

Brecks’ cellar door opens on Mondays and Fridays, and stays open whenever there’s an event on.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door

Monday and Friday, 10am to 2pm, and open during events such as Slow Sundays and Friday nights.

What’s on

Slow Sundays

Carrying on next year, perhaps with a short break in January. Dates go on the website.

What’s on

Friday nights in the barn

The last-Friday evenings carry on through spring and summer.

What’s on

In-depth tastings

A new one every six weeks; the next is on Saturday 12 December. After the success of last month’s flavour tasting, another is planned for the new year.

Getting there

Car or taxi is the only realistic option. The vineyard is on a major cycle route, if you’re happy to ride both ways.

Common questions

When is the Brecks Vineyard cellar door open?

Monday and Friday, 10am to 2pm, and during events like Slow Sundays.

Can you get to Brecks Vineyard without a car?

Only by taxi or bike; it’s on a major cycle route.

When is the next Brecks Vineyard tasting?

Saturday 12 December, with in-depth tastings roughly every six weeks.
