Haywards Heath & Ditchling

Sugrue South Downs

Wivelsfield Green · East Sussex

Estate-verified · November 2026

A wine estate at Wivelsfield Green with tours and tastings three days a week, Cellar Table dinners with guest chefs, and Sugrue Sundays lunches in summer.

The visit

Sugrue runs tours three days a week for small groups of eight; it closes for harvest in September and October.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours and tastings

Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays at 11am or 2:30pm, right through the winter and into 2027. Closed for harvest in September and October.

Confirmed by the estate

Group size

Eight guests on regular tours and tastings; private groups of up to 12.

What’s on

Cellar Table dinners

Mark Hix cooks on 18 December. In 2027 the dinners carry on with Louisa Mokadem from January to April and Mark Hix from May to September. Both can also be booked privately.

What’s on

Sugrue Sundays

The alfresco lunches are back next summer, with dates out in February 2027.

Getting there

The easiest way is a taxi from Haywards Heath station.

Common questions

When are tours at Sugrue South Downs?

Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays at 11am or 2:30pm, except in September and October during harvest.

How big are Sugrue South Downs tour groups?

Eight guests, or up to 12 for a private group.

What is the nearest station to Sugrue South Downs?

Haywards Heath, then a taxi.
