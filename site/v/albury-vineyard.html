Albury Organic Vineyard

Silent Pool, Albury, near Guildford · Surrey

Estate-verified · October 2026

An organic vineyard right on the North Downs Way by the Silent Pool, with Saturday tours, summer wine and cheese tastings, and dog-friendly Walkies & Wine days.

The visit

Albury is a weekend visit from March, and walkers on the North Downs Way are welcome to drop in for a glass.

Confirmed by the estate

Tasting Room

Closed in January and February, then open at weekends from March. This November and December Albury is trialling weekday shop opening, 11am to 3pm.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours and tastings

Tours are always on Saturdays. The Wine & Cheese Tasting runs from March to December, which isn’t the same season as the tours.

Confirmed by the estate

North Downs Way walkers

Walkers can drop in, or book a table if they’d rather.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Dog friendly all the time: bring your dog whenever you visit.

What’s on

Walkies & Wine

The dog-friendly day ran twice this year, in May and October, and is likely to be back next year, maybe with a third.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

Albury suggests places that pour its wine: Barnett Hill, Wotton House or the Harbour Hotel, and the pubs The Drummond in Albury and the Percy Arms in Chilworth.

Common questions

When is Albury Vineyard open?

At weekends from March; the Tasting Room is closed in January and February.

Can North Downs Way walkers stop at Albury?

Yes, walkers can drop in for a glass, or book a table.

Does Albury Vineyard welcome dogs?

Yes, dogs are welcome any time, and it also runs dog-friendly Walkies & Wine days.
