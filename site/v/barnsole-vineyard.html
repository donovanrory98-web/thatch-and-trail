Kent · Canterbury & the North Downs

Barnsole Vineyard

Staple / Canterbury · Kent

A vineyard at Staple near Canterbury where you can drop in for a free tasting Thursday to Sunday, or book a one-hour tour. Dogs and children welcome.

The visit

From Barnsole Vineyard’s own website, October 2026. We’re checking these details with the estate, so confirm before you travel.

Opening

Thursday to Sunday

Open Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 4pm, plus bank holidays. Other times by appointment; call ahead in poor weather.

Tastings and tours

Drop in or book

Drop in for a free tasting, or book the one-hour tour: a walk among the vines, a look inside the winery and a tasting flight.

Food

Kentish platters to order

Order a Kentish cheese or charcuterie platter in advance. Picnics aren’t permitted.

Dogs and children

Both welcome

Dogs and children are welcome.

Getting there

Barnsole is on Fleming Road, Staple, near Canterbury. Ask the vineyard about getting there without a car.

Common questions

When is Barnsole Vineyard open?

Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 4pm, plus bank holidays.

Do you need to book a tasting at Barnsole?

No, you can drop in for a free tasting. The one-hour tour is booked by phone or email.

Are dogs and children welcome at Barnsole?

Yes, both.
