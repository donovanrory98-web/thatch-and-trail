Greyfriars Vineyard

Puttenham, near Guildford · Surrey

Estate-verified · October 2026

A vineyard on the Hog’s Back near Guildford, with guided tours from March, a self-guided trail, and a Horsebox Bar on summer Saturdays.

The visit

Greyfriars is a spring-to-autumn visit for tours, with the cellar door shop and self-guided trail for an easier drop-in.
