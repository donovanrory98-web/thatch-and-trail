Cornwall · South West

Camel Valley

near Bodmin · Cornwall

Estate-verified · October 2026

A family vineyard on the Camel Trail near Bodmin: a weekday shop and wine by the glass, a daily 10:30 tour in summer, and Wednesday evening Grand Tours.

The visit

Camel Valley is a weekday visit: the shop and tours run Monday to Friday, and you can arrive on foot or by bike along the Camel Trail.

Confirmed by the estate

Shop and wine by the glass

Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm; closed at weekends.

Confirmed by the estate

Daily Guided Tour

At 10:30am, Monday to Friday, April to September, then Mondays and Fridays through the winter.

Confirmed by the estate

The Grand Tour

On Wednesday evenings, May to September.

Confirmed by the estate

Places to stay

The vineyard no longer has cottages to book.

Getting there

The estate suggests Bodmin General station, then a walk along the Camel Trail. Sometimes you can take the Bodmin steam railway to Boscarne Junction and walk a short way along the trail. It’s also easy to reach by bike on the Camel Trail from Bodmin, Wadebridge or Padstow.

Visit Camel Valley’s website →

Common questions

Is Camel Valley open at weekends?

No. The shop and wine by the glass are Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm.

When are Camel Valley’s tours?

10:30am Monday to Friday from April to September, and Mondays and Fridays in winter. The Grand Tour runs on Wednesday evenings, May to September.

Can you get to Camel Valley without a car?

Yes: walk or cycle the Camel Trail, from Bodmin General station or from Bodmin, Wadebridge or Padstow.
