Dorset · South West

Little Waddon Vineyard

near Portesham, Weymouth · Dorset

Estate-verified · November 2026

A small vineyard above the Jurassic Coast near Weymouth, where you can stay all year in The Hide, a romantic shepherd’s hut and cabin for two among the vines.

The visit

Little Waddon is pausing its Tour & Taste sessions in 2027 to build more places to stay, so the way to visit is to stay over.

Confirmed by the estate

Tour & Taste: paused in 2027

Tours and tastings are on pause for 2027 while the vineyard builds more accommodation.

Confirmed by the estate

The Hide

A romantic getaway for two, open all year round. Book through the Stay page on the vineyard’s own website. A second spot on the vineyard is planned.

Confirmed by the estate

Buying wine

Buy online all year and choose Click-and-Collect from the vineyard, by arrangement.

Getting there

By car, head for what3words ///leap.winemaker.hatter. Without a car, the X53 bus from Weymouth to Portesham is an option, then about 1.3 miles on foot.

Common questions

Is Little Waddon running tours in 2027?

No, Tour & Taste is paused for 2027 while more accommodation is built.

Can you stay at Little Waddon Vineyard?

Yes, in The Hide, a shepherd’s hut and cabin for two, open all year. Book on the vineyard’s website.

Can you collect wine from Little Waddon?

Yes, order online and choose Click-and-Collect, by arrangement.
