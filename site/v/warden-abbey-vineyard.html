Warden Abbey Vineyard

Old Warden, near Biggleswade · Bedfordshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

A Bedfordshire vineyard at Old Warden, looked after with the Friends of Warden Abbey Vineyard, with public tours from May to September and picnic afternoons and an open day when you can walk the vines yourself.

The visit

Warden Abbey opens through tours and events rather than a cellar door. Public tours run from May to September, and next year’s calendar will be on the vineyard’s website shortly.

Confirmed by the estate

Public tours

May to September, at 11am or 2pm on various Tuesdays, Fridays and Sundays, plus 7pm evening tours on various Thursdays. The 2027 dates will be on the website shortly.

Confirmed by the estate

Private groups

Private tours for groups of more than 12.

Confirmed by the estate

Walking the vines yourself

The self-guided trail can only be used at events, such as the picnic afternoons and the open day.

Confirmed by the estate

Buying wine

Collect from the vineyard on Tuesday or Friday mornings, buy at tours and events (the open day and the Christmas wine sale), or find it at Jordans Mill (Biggleswade), Cambridge Wine (Ampthill) and Franklyns Farm Shop (Thorncote Green). The pink sparkling was a special and has sold out.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

Dogs are welcome. Children are welcome at daytime events, but not on tours or at evening events.

Getting there

Warden Abbey isn’t easy to reach without a car. The nearest stations are Biggleswade and Bedford, and there’s no passing bus, so take a taxi from the station. The nearest village is Old Warden.

Common questions

When are the tours at Warden Abbey Vineyard?

From May to September, at 11am or 2pm on various Tuesdays, Fridays and Sundays, with 7pm tours on various Thursdays. The 2027 dates will be on the vineyard’s website.

Can you walk round Warden Abbey Vineyard on your own?

Only at events, such as the picnic afternoons and the open day, when the self-guided trail is open.

Where can you buy Warden Abbey wine?

At the vineyard on Tuesday or Friday mornings, at tours and events, or from Jordans Mill in Biggleswade, Cambridge Wine in Ampthill and Franklyns Farm Shop at Thorncote Green.

Are dogs allowed at Warden Abbey Vineyard?

Yes, dogs are welcome. Children are welcome at daytime events but not on tours or at evening events.
