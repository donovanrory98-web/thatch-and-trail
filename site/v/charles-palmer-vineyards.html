The Rother Wine Triangle

Charles Palmer Vineyards

Winchelsea · East Sussex

Estate-verified · October 2026

A family estate on 120-acre Wickham Manor at Winchelsea, with a cellar door open Wednesday to Sunday, bar snacks and picnic hampers, a manor stay, and dogs welcome on leads.

The visit

The cellar door is open Wednesday to Sunday; hours may be shorter in winter, and the estate keeps Google up to date.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door hours

Wednesday to Saturday, 11am to 5pm, and Sundays 12 to 4pm. Open until 31 December 2026; hours may reduce over the winter, and the estate keeps Google up to date.

Confirmed by the estate

Food

Bar snacks and boarded picnic hampers. The estate also works with local chefs and can arrange private dining.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Very dog friendly: dogs on leads while on site.

From their website

Staying at Wickham Manor

There’s accommodation on the estate, in the 16th-century manor.

Getting there

Nearest station: Rye, about 10 minutes away by taxi.

Common questions

When is Charles Palmer’s cellar door open?

Wednesday to Saturday 11am to 5pm, and Sundays 12 to 4pm. Hours may be shorter in winter; check Google.

Is there food at Charles Palmer Vineyards?

Yes: bar snacks and boarded picnic hampers, and private dining can be arranged.

Are dogs welcome at Charles Palmer Vineyards?

Yes, very: dogs on leads while on site.

What’s the nearest station to Charles Palmer Vineyards?

Rye, about 10 minutes away by taxi.
