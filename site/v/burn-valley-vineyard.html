Burn Valley Vineyard

North Norfolk · Norfolk

Estate-verified · October 2026

A North Norfolk vineyard with a cellar door, a half-hour self-guided walk round the vines, Saturday guided tastings, and a wine bar reopening around Easter.

The visit

Burn Valley’s wine bar is closed for the winter and reopens around Easter; the cellar door is open for tastings and shop sales from February.
