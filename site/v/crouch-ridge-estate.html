Crouch Ridge Estate

Althorne, near Burnham-on-Crouch · Essex

Estate-verified · October 2026

An Essex estate a seven-minute walk from Althorne station, with a Wine Barn and terrace open at weekends from March to October and cottages on site.

The visit

Crouch Ridge is one of the easiest Essex vineyards to reach by train, and children and dogs on leads are welcome.

Confirmed by the estate

Wine Barn and Terrace

Open Friday to Sunday, 11am to 6pm, March to October. In winter, the wine shop opens on Saturdays in December, 12 to 5pm, and private tours, bookings and events are taken.

Confirmed by the estate

Grand Tour & Tasting

At 11am on dates from 1 May to 26 September 2027. Private tours and tastings for groups of 10 or more run any day from 1 March to 1 November.

Confirmed by the estate

Children and dogs

Children and dogs on leads are welcome.

Confirmed by the estate

Staying

The cottages and Mal’s Barn are booked through Cottages.com.

Getting there

From Althorne station, walk up Station Road to the lower Vineyard & Meadow car park on your right (about 4 minutes), then follow the grassy paths up through the meadow and vines to the Terrace and Wine Barn (about 3 minutes).

Common questions

Can you walk to Crouch Ridge from the station?

Yes, it’s about seven minutes from Althorne station: up Station Road, then through the meadow and vines to the Wine Barn.

Is Crouch Ridge open in winter?

The wine shop opens on Saturdays in December, 12 to 5pm, and private tours and events are taken over the winter.

Are dogs allowed at Crouch Ridge?

Yes, on leads, and children are welcome too.
