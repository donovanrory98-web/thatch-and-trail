East Grinstead, Tunbridge Wells & the High Weald

Busi Jacobsohn Wine Estate

Eridge, outside Tunbridge Wells · East Sussex

Estate-verified · October 2026

A small family estate in the High Weald. Phone ahead for the cellar door, or book a bespoke tasting with the owners for a group of six or more.

The visit

Busi Jacobsohn is a small business, so plan ahead: ring before you call in, as the team may be out in the vineyard or the fields.

Confirmed by the estate

Phone before you visit the cellar door

There are no set days that work best. Just phone before you come: as a small business, the team may be out in the vineyard or the fields.

Groups

Bespoke tastings with the owners

Bespoke tastings are hosted by the owners for a minimum of six people, booked by email. No food is served.

Open days

Announced around May or June

If the estate holds open days next year, they’ll be announced around May or June.

Getting there

The estate is just outside Tunbridge Wells: take the train to Tunbridge Wells or Eridge, then a taxi.

Common questions

Can you visit Busi Jacobsohn Wine Estate?

Yes, but phone before you come to the cellar door, as the team may be out in the vineyard. Bespoke tastings for six or more are booked by email.

Does Busi Jacobsohn have open days?

If there are open days next year, they’ll be announced around May or June.

Is there food at Busi Jacobsohn?

Not at the bespoke tastings, which the owners host without food.

How do you get to Busi Jacobsohn without a car?

Take a taxi from Eridge station or from Tunbridge Wells.
