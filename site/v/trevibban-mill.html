Trevibban Mill

near Padstow · Cornwall

Estate-verified · November 2026

A Cornish vineyard near Padstow, with guided wine tours from April to November and Barnaby’s restaurant downstairs.

The visit

Trevibban Mill opens most of the year, and Barnaby’s restaurant runs Thursday to Sunday through the winter.

From their website

Opening hours

Thursday to Saturday, 12 to 5pm, November to April, then Tuesday to Saturday from April.

From their website

Guided Wine Tour

Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday at 3pm, April to November.

Confirmed by the estate

Barnaby’s

The restaurant downstairs opens Thursday to Sunday from November to March.

Confirmed by the estate

Getting there

No bus serves the vineyard at present; the vineyard’s website lists local taxi firms.

Getting there

There’s no bus to the vineyard; use one of the taxi firms listed on Trevibban Mill’s website.

Common questions

Is Barnaby’s open in winter?

Yes, Thursday to Sunday from November to March.

Can you get to Trevibban Mill by bus?

No bus serves it at present; take a taxi.

When are Trevibban Mill’s tours?

Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday at 3pm, April to November.
