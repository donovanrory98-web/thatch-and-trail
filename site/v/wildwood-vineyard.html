East Grinstead, Tunbridge Wells & the High Weald

Wildwood Vineyard

Eridge, outside Tunbridge Wells · East Sussex

Estate-verified · October 2026

Small friendly woodland vineyard reachable by Spa Valley heritage steam train. Bring a picnic; children's nature trail.

The visit

Wildwood opens on most summer weekends, and you’re welcome to bring a picnic.

Confirmed by the estate

Open 10 to 5, most weekends May to September

Wildwood is open 10am to 5pm on most weekends from May to September.

Tours

Tours at 12 and 2

Tours run at 12:00 and 14:00 on Saturdays and Sundays.

Food

Picnics are welcome

You’re welcome to bring a picnic, and there are cheese plates too.

Spring

Bluebells & Wine in April

The April Bluebells & Wine day is expected back next spring, along with the theatre and the concert.

Getting there

Just outside Tunbridge Wells. Nearest station: Eridge (12 min walk), or Tunbridge Wells and a taxi.

Common questions

When is Wildwood Vineyard open?

10am to 5pm on most weekends from May to September.

When are the tours at Wildwood?

At 12:00 and 14:00 on Saturdays and Sundays.

Can you bring a picnic to Wildwood?

Yes. Picnics are welcome, and there are cheese plates too.

What’s the nearest station to Wildwood Vineyard?
