Kent · The Kent Weald

Gusbourne Estate

Appledore · Kent

Estate-verified · October 2026

A wine estate near Appledore with tours all year (except Tuesdays), walk-ins welcome every day at The Nest cellar door, and a self-guided vineyard walk.

The visit

Gusbourne is open every day for walk-ins; just book ahead if you want a tour, and remember there are none on Tuesdays.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours all year, except Tuesdays

All of Gusbourne’s tours run year-round, but there are no tours on Tuesdays. The estate recommends booking tours in advance.

Confirmed by the estate

Walk in any day

Walk-ins are welcome daily at The Nest, the cellar door, which is open 10am to 5pm. The self-guided vineyard walk needs no booking.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay nearby

There’s no accommodation on the estate. Gusbourne recommends Boys Hall and The George in Rye.

What’s on

Fordwich Arms evening in December

The Chef Series evening with the Fordwich Arms is in December.

From their website

Dogs on leads; children outdoors

Dogs on leads are welcome on the self-guided walk, outdoors and on the ground floor of The Nest. Children are welcome on outdoor experiences but not on hosted tastings and tours.

Getting there

Ashford International is the station Gusbourne recommends: take a pre-booked taxi from there. Appledore station is about an hour’s walk from the estate, with no taxis.

Common questions

Is Gusbourne open to visitors?

Yes, every day. Walk-ins are welcome daily at The Nest, open 10am to 5pm.

When do Gusbourne’s tours run?

All year, every day except Tuesday. Booking in advance is recommended.

Where can you stay near Gusbourne?

There’s no accommodation on the estate; Gusbourne recommends Boys Hall and The George in Rye.

Are dogs welcome at Gusbourne?

Yes, on a lead, on the self-guided walk, outdoors and on the ground floor of The Nest.

How do you get to Gusbourne by train?

Ashford International, then a pre-booked taxi. Appledore station is about an hour’s walk away.
