Burnt House Vineyard

Little Finborough, near Stowmarket · Suffolk

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Suffolk vineyard near Stowmarket with a Vineyard Kitchen and Tasting Room open Thursday evenings and Friday to Sunday all year, plus winter talk-and-tasting nights.

The visit

Burnt House keeps the same hours all year, with weekend tours in summer and a monthly tour through the winter.

Confirmed by the estate

Vineyard Kitchen & Tasting Room

Open Thursday evenings and Friday to Sunday, with the same advertised times all year round.

Confirmed by the estate

Tour & Tasting

From April to September: Saturdays at 12pm and 4pm, and every other Sunday at 12pm. From October to March, one a month.

What’s on

Thursday talk-and-tasting nights

Run in the winter months, October to March, while there are fewer tours.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

The estate suggests The Bildeston Crown, The Peacock at Chelsworth and Retreat East.

Common questions

Is Burnt House Vineyard open in winter?

Yes, the Vineyard Kitchen and Tasting Room keep the same hours all year: Thursday evenings and Friday to Sunday.

When are Burnt House’s tours?

April to September on Saturdays at 12pm and 4pm and every other Sunday at 12pm; one a month from October to March.

Where can you stay near Burnt House?

The Bildeston Crown, The Peacock at Chelsworth or Retreat East.
