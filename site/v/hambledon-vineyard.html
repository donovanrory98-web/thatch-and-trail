Hampshire

Hambledon Vineyard

Hambledon · Hampshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A historic Hampshire vineyard with a shop open every day, tours twice daily from March to October, and a restaurant and bar where dogs are welcome.

The visit

Hambledon works all year: the shop opens daily, and tours run every day in the main season, with fewer days in winter.

Confirmed by the estate

Shop open every day

The shop is open Monday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours at 11am and 2pm

From March to October, tours run every day at 11am and 2pm, with a 5pm tour at the height of summer. From October to March there are no tours on Mondays or Tuesdays. Times can vary with the weather.

Confirmed by the estate

Restaurant, bar and courtyard

The restaurant serves lunch (12 to 2pm) and dinner (6 to 8pm) Wednesday to Saturday, and Sunday lunch. The bar serves coffee, drinks and bar snacks Wednesday to Sunday from 11am. The courtyard opens for casual dining from April to September, weather permitting.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

Dogs are welcome in the restaurant and bar, and children are very welcome in the restaurant. Neither can join the tours, for health and safety reasons.

What’s on

Winemaker’s Insight Tour and WSET courses

The Winemaker’s Insight Tour is running again next year; dates are still to be set. Hambledon also runs WSET wine courses.

Getting there

Hambledon has started Defender drop-offs from Petersfield and Havant stations and within five miles. It hopes to keep them going next year, subject to availability.

Common questions

When are Hambledon’s tours?

March to October, every day at 11am and 2pm (plus 5pm in high summer). In winter there are no tours on Mondays or Tuesdays.

Can you bring a dog to Hambledon?

Yes, into the restaurant and bar, but not on tours.

Can you get to Hambledon by train?

Hambledon runs Defender drop-offs from Petersfield and Havant stations, subject to availability.
