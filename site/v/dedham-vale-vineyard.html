Dedham Vale Vineyard

Boxted, near Colchester · Essex

Estate-verified · October 2026

A vineyard in Constable country near Colchester, with Saturday tours and tastings from May, optional picnic boxes, and two cottages opening for bookings in February.

The visit

Dedham Vale is a spring-and-summer visit, built around Saturday tours; the cellar door opens when a tour is on.

Confirmed by the estate

Tour & Tasting

11am to 1:30pm, planned for every Saturday from May. Dates will be posted with the rest of next year’s events.

Confirmed by the estate

Picnic box

Tours run in spring and summer, and you can book with or without a picnic box.

Confirmed by the estate

The cottages

The Wine Maker’s Cottage and Willow Lake Cottage open for bookings from February 2027, for 2027 stays. Details nearer the time.

Confirmed by the estate

Buying wine

The cellar door is open when there’s a tour; otherwise contact the vineyard to arrange to meet at the cellar door.

Getting there

Colchester station is about 45 minutes from London by train; it’s a 15-minute taxi from there.

Common questions

When are Dedham Vale’s tours?

Planned for every Saturday from May, 11am to 1:30pm.

Can you stay at Dedham Vale Vineyard?

Yes: the Wine Maker’s Cottage and Willow Lake Cottage open for bookings in February 2027.

How do you get to Dedham Vale by train?

Colchester station, about 45 minutes from London, then a 15-minute taxi.
