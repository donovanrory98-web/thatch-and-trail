Vineyards / Kent / Simpsons’ Wine Estate

Kent · Canterbury & the North Downs

Simpsons’ Wine Estate

Barham / Canterbury · Kent

Estate-verified · October 2026

A wine estate on the North Downs at Barham with guided tastings through the week, Saturday vineyard and winery tours, and the world’s first winery helter-skelter.

The visit

Everything at Simpsons’ is scheduled and booked online, which keeps groups small.

Confirmed by the estate

Tastings and tours all year

Guided tastings run across the week in the Glass House Tasting Room, and the Vineyard & Winery Tour runs on Saturdays and some Friday afternoons. Tours and tastings run all year, most 2027 dates are already live, and everything is pre-booked online.

Confirmed by the estate

Access

The Glass House is up two flights of stairs; ground-floor tastings are possible if you call ahead. Tours are small and scheduled, so the team can help, and a new visitor space is planned within two years.

Confirmed by the estate

Don’t miss

The helter-skelter slide from the tasting room down to the winery, the first at any winery in the world.

What’s on

Sunset Sessions and a festive evening

Sunset Sessions return on summer Friday evenings next year, with platters from Macknade (dates to come). Style Your Festive Feast is on Thursday 10 December.

From their website

Before you go

Guests must be 18 or over, and dogs aren’t allowed apart from registered assistance dogs.

Getting there

Nearest station: Canterbury West, a short taxi ride away, with hourly high-speed trains from London St Pancras. Local taxis the estate suggests: Nashat Hamdallah Taxis (07920 424372) and Kev’s Taxis Canterbury (07887 668940). The entrance is on Church Lane, opposite St John the Baptist Church.

Common questions

When can you visit Simpsons’ Wine Estate?

Guided tastings run across the week, and vineyard and winery tours on Saturdays and some Friday afternoons. All events are scheduled and need to be pre-booked online in advance.

Is Simpsons’ accessible if you can’t manage stairs?

The tasting room is up two flights of stairs, but ground-floor tastings are possible if you call ahead.

Are dogs or children allowed at Simpsons’?

No. Guests must be 18 or over, and only registered assistance dogs are allowed.

How do you get to Simpsons’ Wine Estate by train?

Canterbury West, then a short taxi; the estate suggests Nashat Hamdallah Taxis or Kev’s Taxis Canterbury.
