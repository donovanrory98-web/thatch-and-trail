Essex · East of England

Saffron Grange

near Saffron Walden · Essex

Estate-verified · November 2026

A sparkling wine estate near Saffron Walden with a cellar door open Thursday to Saturday all winter, tours most of the year, and Sundowners with food trucks in summer.

The visit

Saffron Grange opens Thursday to Saturday through the winter, apart from a Christmas break.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar Door Shop and Tasting Room

Thursday to Saturday, 10am to 4pm, through the winter. Closed from Thursday 24 December to Thursday 14 January. Full hours are on the estate’s contact page.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours most of the year

Tours run in summer, and now in autumn and winter too: smaller and more intimate, with less time outdoors and some time in the winery.

What’s on

Sundowners

Back next summer on Friday evenings, and Sundays too, with food trucks.

Confirmed by the estate

Where to stay

The Crown at Little Walden, very close by, has a couple of rooms. In Saffron Walden: the Cross Keys Hotel, a Premier Inn and the Coach House B&B.

Getting there

Audley End station (direct from Tottenham Hale and London Liverpool Street), then a taxi.

Common questions

Is Saffron Grange open in winter?

Yes, Thursday to Saturday 10am to 4pm, except for a break from 24 December to 14 January.

Does Saffron Grange run winter tours?

Yes, smaller autumn and winter tours with some time in the winery.

How do you get to Saffron Grange by train?

Audley End station, direct from London Liverpool Street, then a taxi.
