Haywards Heath & Ditchling

Newick Wine Estate

Newick · East Sussex

Estate-verified · November 2026

A young estate in Newick with tours on Friday and Saturday mornings from May to September, cheese and charcuterie plates, picnics from 2027, and its first still wines now released.

The visit

Newick’s summer tours are on Fridays and Saturdays at 11:30am; the cellar door opens on other days by appointment.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours in 2027

May to September, on Fridays and Saturdays at 11:30am. Private tours by appointment.

Confirmed by the estate

Food

Cheese and charcuterie plates, and picnics from 2027.

Confirmed by the estate

Cellar door

Open on other days by appointment.

Confirmed by the estate

New wines

The 2025 Pinot Noir came out this autumn. The 2026 still wines follow in spring 2027, and the first sparkling wine at the end of 2027.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Dogs on leads are welcome.

Getting there

There are buses from Haywards Heath station to Newick.
