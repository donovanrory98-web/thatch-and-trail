Vineyards / Hampshire / Exton Park

Hampshire

Exton Park

Exton, Meon Valley · Hampshire

Estate-verified · October 2026

A Meon Valley estate with weekly Friday vineyard tours from March to October and Saturday tours with a seasonal lunch, all looking out over the South Downs.

The visit

Exton Park is a spring-to-autumn visit, built around guided tours that end in the tasting room at Exton Hall.

Confirmed by the estate

Friday tours, March to October

Planned weekly from March to October 2027: a guided walk round the vineyard, then a tasting of the Reserve Blend range in Exton Hall’s tasting room.

Confirmed by the estate

Saturday tours with lunch

Running again in 2027, and more often than this year. The same tour, plus a seasonal lunch in the dining room, with wines by the glass or bottle.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs

Welcome if well behaved and on a lead at all times.

Confirmed by the estate

Don’t miss

The estate’s tip: Exton Hall and its sweeping views of the South Downs.

Getting there

The best stations are Petersfield or Winchester, both with a taxi to the vineyard.

Common questions

When can you tour Exton Park?

Friday tours run weekly from March to October 2027, and Saturday tours with lunch run more often than in 2026.

Can you take a dog to Exton Park?

Yes, if well behaved and on a lead.

Which station is best for Exton Park?

Petersfield or Winchester, then a taxi.
