Brabourne Vineyard

Brabourne, near Ashford · Kent

Estate-verified · November 2026

A tiny two-acre family vineyard near Ashford, with private tours for your group only, Fig Cottage to stay in, and plenty of animals to meet.

The visit

Brabourne is small, friendly and ideal for a first vineyard visit; book a tour so the family knows to expect you.

Confirmed by the estate

Booking a tour

Tours and tastings must be booked: there are no staff, just the family who live on site. Tours are never mixed, run four times a day on Saturdays and Sundays, and last well over an hour. Summer weekends fill up, though a week ahead there are usually a couple of slots. Online booking closes 24 hours before.

Confirmed by the estate

Dropping in

From about May to September, weekends are busy with drop-ins; you can book a table for free to be sure of space. On weekdays, take your chances: if someone’s in, they’ll happily sell you wine or pour a glass.

What’s on

Events

One big open vineyard day in September, plus bookable events such as wellness evenings, afternoon tea and wreath making. Dates go out on social media.

Confirmed by the estate

Fig Cottage

Looking over the vineyard, and still booked through cottages.com.

Confirmed by the estate

Good for families

Lots of poultry and animals (dogs, chickens, ducks), and an easy introduction to English wine. Non-drinkers aren’t charged, so a designated driver is welcome.

Getting there

Wye is the closest station (about 5 miles), but most visitors come from Ashford International (about 7 miles, with a taxi rank, and 37 minutes from St Pancras). Plan taxis ahead; Uber now reaches the area.

Common questions

Do you need to book at Brabourne Vineyard?

Yes for tours and tastings. On summer weekends you can drop in or book a free table; weekday drop-ins are hit and miss.

Can you stay at Brabourne Vineyard?

Yes, in Fig Cottage, booked through cottages.com.

How do you get to Brabourne by train?

Ashford International is about 7 miles away with a taxi rank; Wye is closer but smaller. Book taxis ahead.
