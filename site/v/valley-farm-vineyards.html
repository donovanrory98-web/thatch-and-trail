Valley Farm Vineyards

Wissett, near Halesworth · Suffolk

Estate-verified · November 2026

A Suffolk vineyard near Halesworth with a year-round tour, tasting and build-your-own wood-fired pizza, a cabin in the vines open even at Christmas, and a circular walk from the station.

The visit

Valley Farm is usually open Wednesday to Sunday afternoons, but it’s run by the people who tend the vines, so ring before you set off.

Confirmed by the estate

Opening times

As a guide, Wednesday to Sunday, 12 noon to 4pm. Ring to check before you go: 07920 180712.

Confirmed by the estate

Tour, tasting and pizza

The tour, tasting and build-your-own wood-fired pizza runs all year round.

Confirmed by the estate

Cabin-in-the-Vines

Open all year, even at Christmas and New Year.

Confirmed by the estate

Walking from Halesworth

A 7.5-mile circular walk from Halesworth passes the vineyard, a good option if you come by train.

Getting there

Take the train to Halesworth and follow the 7.5-mile circular walk, which passes the vineyard.

Common questions

When is Valley Farm Vineyards open?

As a guide, Wednesday to Sunday, 12 noon to 4pm. Ring 07920 180712 to check before you go.

Does Valley Farm do food?

Yes, a tour and tasting with build-your-own wood-fired pizza, all year round.

Can you walk to Valley Farm from Halesworth?

Yes, a 7.5-mile circular walk from Halesworth passes the vineyard.
