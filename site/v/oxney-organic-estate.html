The Rother Wine Triangle

Oxney Organic Estate

Beckley / Rye · East Sussex

Estate-verified · August 2026

England’s largest single-estate organic wine producer, outside Rye — wine flights most of the year, Saturday tours through the summer, and beds for 28 across shepherd’s huts, barns and the vineyard house.

The visit

Oxney runs on two tracks: wine flights, which are available most of the year, and guided tours and tastings, which are a summer Saturday affair. Knowing which you’re after is the difference between a good visit and a wasted drive.

Confirmed by the estate

Wine flights — the year-round option

Wine flights run Tuesday to Sunday from May to October, and Tuesday to Saturday from November to April. This is the estate’s own recommendation for a first visit: go for a flight and work through the wines.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours and tastings — Saturdays, May to October

Guided tours and tastings run on Saturdays through the summer season. Outside that, the estate holds paired tastings on specific dates over the winter — worth checking the calendar rather than assuming.

Worth knowing before you go

You can’t wander the vineyard on your own

Unlike several Sussex estates, there’s no self-guided route and no marked trail at Oxney. Seeing the vines means booking onto a tour — so don’t come expecting a free walk through the rows.

The organic side

It’s front and centre, not behind the scenes

The estate talks about organic viticulture and winemaking a great deal on the tour — so if that’s why you’re coming, you’ll get the substance rather than a passing mention.

Getting there

Rye is the station, and the estate confirms it works well. There are plenty of Rye taxis, but book in advance rather than expecting one on the platform. Parking on site if you drive.

Staying the night

The estate sleeps up to 28 people across shepherd’s huts, converted barns and the vineyard house. Rates vary a good deal by season — and shepherd’s hut stays include a tour and tasting, which is the estate’s other pick for a first visit.

Common questions

When can you visit Oxney Organic Estate?

Wine flights run Tuesday to Sunday from May to October, and Tuesday to Saturday from November to April. Guided tours and tastings run on Saturdays from May to October, with paired tastings on specific dates over the winter.

Can you walk around the vineyard at Oxney?

No — there is no self-guided route or marked trail. Seeing the vines means booking onto a tour.

Is Oxney Organic Estate dog-friendly?

Yes — dogs are very welcome.

Can you stay at Oxney Organic Estate?

Yes. The estate sleeps up to 28 people across shepherd’s huts, converted barns and the vineyard house. Rates vary considerably by season, and shepherd’s hut stays include a tour and tasting.

What is the nearest station to Oxney Organic Estate?

Rye. There are plenty of taxis in Rye but the estate advises booking in advance. There is parking on site if you drive.

What should a first-time visitor to Oxney do?

The estate suggests going for a wine flight to discover the wines, or staying the night in a shepherd’s hut.
