Ceredigion · Wales

Llaethliw

Neuadd Lwyd, near Aberaeron · Ceredigion

Estate-verified · November 2026

A family-run vineyard in Ceredigion, with a dog-friendly cottage to stay in all year, a cellar door, and its own Hansh cider.

The visit

Llaethliw is run by a family who all work other jobs too, so scheduled tours are for groups of 12 or more; the cottage and cellar door are the easy way in.

Confirmed by the estate

Tours and tastings

Scheduled tours are for groups of 12 or more.

Confirmed by the estate

Vineyard Cottage

Bookable all year round, and all dogs are welcome. More accommodation is on the way.

Confirmed by the estate

Buying wine and cider

Buy direct at the cellar door. Hansh cider is on sale too, and can be added to a group tour.

Getting there

There’s a bus stop at the bottom of the road, or take a local taxi.

Visit Llaethliw’s website →

Common questions

Can you tour Llaethliw?

Scheduled tours are for groups of 12 or more.

Is Llaethliw’s cottage dog friendly?

Yes, all dogs are welcome, and it’s bookable all year.

Can you buy Llaethliw wine on site?

Yes, at the cellar door, along with Hansh cider.
