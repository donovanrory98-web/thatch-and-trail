Gutter & Stars

Chesterton Mill, Cambridge · Cambridgeshire

Estate-verified · November 2026

An urban winery in Cambridge with winemaker-led Thursday evening tastings twice a month, open days in spring, summer and at Christmas, and visits by arrangement.

Chesterton Mill, home of the winery · Photograph courtesy of Gutter & Stars

The visit

Gutter & Stars is a Thursday-evening visit; outside the tastings and open days, drop the winemaker a line and he’ll make a plan.

Confirmed by the estate

Thursday tastings

Every other Thursday, 6:30 to 7:30pm: five wines led by the winemaker, about two a month all year. 2027 starts with 14 and 28 January, 11 and 25 February and 25 March.

What’s on

Open days

Christmas at Gutter&Stars on Saturday 5 December, 12 to 5pm, free with no booking. Next year there’s a spring open day in May and a summer one in July.

Confirmed by the estate

By arrangement

Wine sales and tastings are otherwise by appointment, but it’s very flexible: get in touch and they’ll find a time.

Confirmed by the estate

Dogs and children

Dogs can come to tastings. No children at tastings, but they’re very welcome at open days.

Getting there

By train to Cambridge or Cambridge North.

Common questions

When are Gutter & Stars’ tastings?

Every other Thursday, 6:30 to 7:30pm, about twice a month all year.

Can children go to Gutter & Stars?

Not to the tastings, but they’re welcome at open days.

How do you get to Gutter & Stars by train?

Cambridge or Cambridge North stations.
