New · East of England

East of England vineyards you can visit

Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk, Hertfordshire, Cambridgeshire and Bedfordshire, from the Crouch to the Broads, Cambridge and Old Warden. 15 estates so far, and every one has confirmed its details with us by email: opening days, tours, food, dogs and how to get there. Hours change with the seasons, so check with the estate before you travel.

Hertfordshire
Bailey Hills
near Bishop’s Stortford
VerifiedFood
↦ Bishop’s Stortford (6–7 min taxi)

Estate-verified: A Hertfordshire vineyard a short taxi from Bishop’s Stortford, with tours and tastings every Friday and Saturday all year, Friday evenings until 9pm, and summer food vans.

VerifiedFoodStay

New Hall Wine Estate
Purleigh, near Maldon
Verified
↦ Wickford or South Woodham Ferrers, then taxi

Estate-verified: An Essex estate with Cellar Door Saturdays from Easter to September, Friday Tour & Tastings, festive shopping days in November and December, and shepherd huts next door.
Essex
Saffron Grange
near Saffron Walden
Verified
↦ Audley End, then taxi

Estate-verified: A sparkling wine estate near Saffron Walden with a cellar door open Thursday to Saturday all winter, tours most of the year, and Sundowners with food trucks in summer.
