Three vineyards from Pulborough

Seven Sussex vineyards sit within twenty minutes of Pulborough station. Three of them make an unhurried day on foot and by taxi — and one of them does lunch.

A day, no car

  1. 1
    Stopham Vineyard

    Pulborough (5 min)

    Most Saturdays

  2. 2
    Kinsbrook Vineyard

    Pulborough (15 min) · food on site · dogs welcome

    Thu–Sun (café 7 days)

  3. 3
    Nutbourne Vineyard

    Pulborough (10 min)

    Mon–Sat (tours Sat May–Sep)

How the day runs

Start at Stopham, five minutes from the platform and the closest vineyard to any station in Sussex. Kinsbrook is the middle of the day — it's the only one of the three with a kitchen, so lunch happens here whether you planned it or not. Finish at Nutbourne, ten minutes back toward the station, where the walk up to the old windmill is the view people remember.

Before you go

Opening days shift with the season and several of these need booking. Check each estate before you commit to the train — we list what we've confirmed, but vineyards change their minds about Sundays.
