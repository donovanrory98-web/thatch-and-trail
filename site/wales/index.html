Wales vineyards you can visit

From the Vale of Glamorgan to Pembrokeshire and Ceredigion. 6 estates so far, and every one has confirmed its details with us by email: opening days, tours, food, dogs and how to get there. Hours change with the seasons, so check with the estate before you travel.

Wales 2 vineyards · St Hilary, Vale of Glamorgan

Wales
Glyndwr Vineyard
near Cowbridge, Vale of Glamorgan
VerifiedStay
↦ X2 to Clare Garden Village (5-minute walk)

Estate-verified: Wales’ oldest vineyard, in the Vale of Glamorgan, where the two-hour Saturday tour comes with a two-course lunch and a visit to the llamas.
Wales
St Hilary Vineyard
St Hilary
VerifiedStay
↦ A48 stop, then a 10-minute walk

Estate-verified: A Vale of Glamorgan vineyard open for pre-booked tours from late May to early November, with supper clubs through the season, harvest volunteering, and a dog-friendly barn to stay in.

Monmouthshire 2 vineyards · Abergavenny, Raglan

Monmouthshire
The Dell Vineyard
Raglan
Verified
↦ Abergavenny (15 min taxi)

Estate-verified: A Monmouthshire vineyard near Raglan, open at weekends in spring and summer, with Sunday and Saturday afternoon tours and monthly food and live music days.
Monmouthshire
White Castle Vineyard
Llanvetherine, near Abergavenny
Verified
↦ Abergavenny (5 miles)

Estate-verified: A family vineyard near Abergavenny, a mile from White Castle, open Friday to Sunday in summer and Saturdays in winter, with a monthly Deluxe Tour and summer supper clubs.

Pembrokeshire 1 vineyard · Narberth

Pembrokeshire
Velfrey Vineyard
Lampeter Velfrey, near Narberth
Verified
↦ Whitland (pre-book a taxi, or 30-min walk)

Estate-verified: A Pembrokeshire vineyard near Narberth with 90-minute tours twice a week, guided tastings all year, sharing boards, and a welcome for children and dogs.

Ceredigion 1 vineyard · Aberaeron

Ceredigion
Llaethliw
Neuadd Lwyd, near Aberaeron
VerifiedStay
↦ Stop at the bottom of the road

Estate-verified: A family-run vineyard in Ceredigion, with a dog-friendly cottage to stay in all year, a cellar door, and its own Hansh cider.
