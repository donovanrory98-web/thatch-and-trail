Common questions

How many vineyards can you visit in East Sussex?

26 vineyards in East Sussex welcome visitors, and 17 of them have confirmed their details with Thatch & Trail directly.

Which East Sussex vineyards serve food?

10 have food on site, including Artelium Wine Estate, Beacon Down Vineyard, Court Garden Vineyard, Mousehall Distillery & Vineyard, Off The Line Vineyard.

Where are the vineyards in East Sussex?

The High Weald, the Rother valley, the Downs above Lewes and the coast near Rye. Towns with a vineyard nearby include Alfriston / Seaford, Ashdown Forest / Uckfield, Beckley / Rye, Blackboys, Ditchling, Eridge, outside Tunbridge Wells, Furners Green / Uckfield, Heathfield.
