Questions about the Rother Wine Triangle

What is the Rother Wine Triangle?

It is the cluster of vineyards around Rye, Robertsbridge and Winchelsea in the Rother district of East Sussex. This trail covers 7 estates there, from Oxney Organic Estate and Tillingham Estate near Rye to Carr Taylor Vineyard outside Hastings.

How many vineyards can you visit in the Rother Wine Triangle?

7 estates welcome visitors: Oxney Organic Estate, Tillingham Estate, Oastbrook Estate, Charles Palmer Vineyards, Sedlescombe Organic, Mountfield Winery and Carr Taylor Vineyard.

Which Rother Wine Triangle vineyards serve food?

Tillingham Estate and Sedlescombe Organic have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in the Rother Wine Triangle?

Yes: Oxney Organic Estate, Tillingham Estate, Oastbrook Estate, Charles Palmer Vineyards, Sedlescombe Organic and Mountfield Winery have rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to Rother Wine Triangle vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Rye (book a taxi ahead) for Oxney Organic Estate; Rye (taxi) for Tillingham Estate; Robertsbridge (15 min) for Oastbrook Estate; Rye (10 min) for Charles Palmer Vineyards; Battle (estate’s recommendation) for Sedlescombe Organic; Robertsbridge (8 min) for Mountfield Winery; Hastings for Carr Taylor Vineyard.

Getting to the Rother Wine Triangle

The vineyards here are reached from Hastings, Robertsbridge and Rye. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
