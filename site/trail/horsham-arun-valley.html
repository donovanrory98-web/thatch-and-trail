Private vineyard tours near Horsham

Two estates here have confirmed their private tours with us by email:

  • Kinsbrook Vineyard: private tours at £40 per person, arranged for any day and any time you like. There’s also a private tasting with dinner on Friday and Saturday evenings.
  • Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards): a Private Tour and Tasting led by the owner or winemaker, with availability almost every day of the year. Book online.

Questions about Horsham & the Arun Valley

Which vineyards are in the Arun Valley?

5 of the 8 sit around Pulborough, where the Arun valley opens out: Nyetimber, Nutbourne Vineyard, Kinsbrook Vineyard, Stopham Vineyard and Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards).

Can you book a private vineyard tour near Horsham?

Yes. Kinsbrook Vineyard has confirmed private tours at £40 per person, on any day and at any time you arrange. Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards) offers a Private Tour and Tasting with the owner or winemaker, almost every day of the year; book online.

How many vineyards can you visit in Horsham & the Arun Valley?

8 estates welcome visitors: Nyetimber, Bolney Wine Estate, Nutbourne Vineyard, Leonardslee Wine Estate, Kinsbrook Vineyard, Stopham Vineyard, Albourne Estate and Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards).

Which Horsham & Arun Valley vineyards serve food?

Nyetimber, Bolney Wine Estate, Leonardslee Wine Estate and Kinsbrook Vineyard have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in Horsham & the Arun Valley?

Yes: Leonardslee Wine Estate has rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to Horsham & Arun Valley vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Pulborough (15 min) for Nyetimber; Haywards Heath (15 min) for Bolney Wine Estate; Pulborough (10 min, pre-book) for Nutbourne Vineyard; 45 min from London + 10 min taxi for Leonardslee Wine Estate; Billingshurst (nearest) for Kinsbrook Vineyard; Pulborough (5 min) for Stopham Vineyard; Hassocks (10 min) for Albourne Estate; Pulborough (10 min) for Ambriel (Redfold Vineyards).

Getting to the Horsham & the Arun Valley

The vineyards here are reached from Hassocks, Haywards Heath, Horsham and Pulborough. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
