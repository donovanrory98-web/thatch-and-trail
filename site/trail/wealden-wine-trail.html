Questions about the Wealden Wine Trail

Where is the Wealden Wine Trail?

It runs across the Wealden district of East Sussex, from Ashdown Forest near Uckfield down to Rathfinny Wine Estate near Alfriston on the South Downs. It takes in 12 estates.

How many vineyards can you visit in the Wealden Wine Trail?

12 estates welcome visitors: Rathfinny Wine Estate, Bluebell Vineyard Estate, Beacon Down Vineyard, Coes Farm, Mousehall Distillery & Vineyard, Winklestone Vineyard, Henners Vineyard, Hidden Spring, Tickerage Vineyard, Off The Line Vineyard, Tilsmore and The Secret Vineyard.

Which Wealden Wine Trail vineyards serve food?

Rathfinny Wine Estate and Beacon Down Vineyard have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site, and Mousehall Distillery & Vineyard serves cheese and charcuterie sharing boards.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in the Wealden Wine Trail?

Yes: Rathfinny Wine Estate, Coes Farm and Mousehall Distillery & Vineyard have rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to Wealden Wine Trail vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Seaford (10 min) for Rathfinny Wine Estate; Haywards Heath/Uckfield (20 min) for Bluebell Vineyard Estate; Uckfield (15 min) for Beacon Down Vineyard; Uckfield (20 min) for Coes Farm; Wadhurst (10 min drive) for Mousehall Distillery & Vineyard; Buxted (short taxi) or Uckfield (bus 29) for Winklestone Vineyard; Polegate for Henners Vineyard.

Getting to the Wealden Wine Trail

The vineyards here are reached from Haywards Heath/Uckfield, Polegate, Seaford, Uckfield and Wadhurst. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
