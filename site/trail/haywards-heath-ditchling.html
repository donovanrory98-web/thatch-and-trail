Questions about Haywards Heath & Ditchling

How many vineyards can you visit in Haywards Heath & Ditchling?

7 estates welcome visitors: Ridgeview Wine Estate, Artelium Wine Estate, Highweald Wine Estate, Court Garden Vineyard, Everflyht, Newick Wine Estate and Sugrue South Downs.

Which Haywards Heath & Ditchling vineyards serve food?

Ridgeview Wine Estate, Highweald Wine Estate and Artelium Wine Estate have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site, and Court Garden Vineyard serves picnic lunches on Saturdays from May to September.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in Haywards Heath & Ditchling?

Not on the estates themselves yet. Stay in a nearby town and taxi out.

Can you get to Haywards Heath & Ditchling vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Burgess Hill (10 min) for Ridgeview Wine Estate; Plumpton (30 min walk) or Burgess Hill (taxi) for Artelium Wine Estate; Haywards Heath for Highweald Wine Estate; Hassocks for Court Garden Vineyard; Lewes for Everflyht.

Getting to the Haywards Heath & Ditchling

The vineyards here are reached from Burgess Hill, Hassocks, Haywards Heath, Lewes and Plumpton. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
