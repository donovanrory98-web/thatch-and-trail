Questions about Chichester & Petworth

How many vineyards can you visit in Chichester & Petworth?

6 estates welcome visitors: Tinwood Estate, Ashling Park Estate, Roebuck Estates, Upperton Vineyards, Blackdown Ridge Estate and Weyborne.

Which Chichester & Petworth vineyards serve food?

Tinwood Estate and Ashling Park Estate have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in Chichester & Petworth?

Yes: Tinwood Estate and Ashling Park Estate have rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to Chichester & Petworth vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Chichester (10 min) for Tinwood Estate; Nutbourne (8 min) for Ashling Park Estate; Pulborough or Haslemere (15 min) for Roebuck Estates; Pulborough (15 min) for Upperton Vineyards; Haslemere (15 min) for Blackdown Ridge Estate.

Getting to the Chichester & Petworth

The vineyards here are reached from Chichester, Haslemere, Nutbourne and Pulborough. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
