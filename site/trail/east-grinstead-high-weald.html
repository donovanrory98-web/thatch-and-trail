Questions about East Grinstead, Tunbridge Wells & the High Weald

How many vineyards can you visit in East Grinstead, Tunbridge Wells & the High Weald?

4 estates welcome visitors: Kingscote Estate, Busi Jacobsohn Wine Estate, Wildwood Vineyard and Rowfant Vineyard.

Which East Grinstead & High Weald vineyards serve food?

Kingscote Estate has a restaurant, café or kitchen on site.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in East Grinstead, Tunbridge Wells & the High Weald?

Yes: Kingscote Estate has rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to East Grinstead & High Weald vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: East Grinstead for Kingscote Estate; Eridge (5 min) for Busi Jacobsohn Wine Estate; Eridge (12 min walk) for Wildwood Vineyard.
