Questions about Worthing & the Downs

How many vineyards can you visit in Worthing & the Downs?

2 estates welcome visitors: Wiston Estate and Highdown Vineyard.

Which Worthing & the Downs vineyards serve food?

Wiston Estate and Highdown Vineyard have a restaurant, café or kitchen on site.

Can you stay overnight at a vineyard in Worthing & the Downs?

Yes: Wiston Estate has rooms, lodges or cottages on the estate.

Can you get to Worthing & the Downs vineyards by train?

Yes, usually with a short taxi from the station. Nearest stations: Worthing/Horsham (20 min) for Wiston Estate; Goring-by-Sea (10 min) for Highdown Vineyard.

Getting to the Worthing & the Downs

The vineyards here are reached from Goring-by-Sea and Worthing/Horsham. None of them are walkable from the platform bar one — budget for a taxi at each end, or a driver who's prepared to spit.
